How it stays light. Sky, hills, water and palms are painted once into a still image and repainted only when the hour, season or weather changes. Only rain, ripples, fireflies, stars and clouds move, capped at 30 frames a second, and the loop pauses when the page is hidden. At Balanced nothing moves and the scene repaints once a minute. At Essential it is a flat colour. A teacher can always choose Plain or a solid colour instead. Frost is not a live blur: each time the still scene is repainted, a tiny blurred copy is made once and the cards show their slice of it, so it costs the same on a cheap phone as on a new one. Look up, Sky first and Settle when idle only move and fade the app, which is nearly free. Sky colours, season timing and sunrise for Colombo are approximate sketches, not yet in the app.
