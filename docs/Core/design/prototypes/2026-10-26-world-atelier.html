What makes it this world

    One baseline for every world

    Old and new worlds fill in the same kit. The roles, the type scale, the spacing and the contrast rules are fixed; a world only chooses how each role looks. That is what lets every screen follow any world without screen-by-screen work.

    Type roles

    Display 38, title 20, body 15, caption 13.5 italic or plain, label 11, numerals tabular. Each role names a family and its variable axes.

    Colour roles

    Ground, raised, sunken, three inks, lamp, on-lamp, glow, ornament, in light and dark. Ink 7:1 on ground, soft ink 4.5:1 on raised, on-lamp 4.5:1, lamp 3:1 as a shape.

    Shape roles

    Corners for control, surface, chip, field, switch and dot. An edge (hairline, frame, double, accent or none), a line weight and style, and an optional cut corner.

    Voice and scene

    Label style, chip style, field style, main button fill, one ornament, and a backdrop set of three views that turns daily at dawn.
