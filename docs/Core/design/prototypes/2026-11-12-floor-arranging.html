Floor Arranging

Put away · drag here to hide, drag out to bring back

Lift a piece, and the floor makes room.

Hold any piece on the phone. It lifts under your finger, and the pieces around it slide out of the way while you move. Drop it into another group to move it there. Drag a group's handle and the floor zooms out so whole groups can be reordered. When you let go, it zooms back in.

There are no arrows on screen. Tapping a piece while arranging opens a short "Move to" sheet. A keyboard uses Space to lift, the arrow keys to move and Space to drop, and a screen reader hears every new place. So nobody needs to drag.

Motion, as verger would set it

Rich adds a spring, a lift shadow and a slight tilt. Balanced keeps the slide and drops the rest. Essential has no motion: places swap at once, and an outline replaces the shadow.

What each gesture saves

  • Move inside a groupSaves the new order with dropSurface(id, visible, index). Hidden pieces stay where they were.
  • Drop in another groupSaves the new group with setSurfaceZone, then the order.
  • Drag a group's handleZooms out to bands, and saves the group order with setGroupOrder.
  • Drop on "Put away"Hides the piece with setSurfaceHidden(id, true). Dragging it back out undoes that.
  • UndoPuts back the layout from before the move, as a single edit.
