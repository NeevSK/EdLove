Gurudevi World Model
Gurudevi · design, Nov 2025 to Nov 2026

One world, six questions

Every screen on web, mobile and explore.gurudevi.app is one answer to: which world, what hour, which place, what backdrop, how rich the phone can afford, and what the person is doing. One resolver turns the answers into a scene, and each platform only paints it.

Seams

Where the docs and the code split

24 Feb to 9 Jun 2026The living backdrop

Mobile had a phase-crossfading sky behind every tab. It was dropped when navigation moved to Expo Router.

Oct 2026Web ahead of mobile

Wallpapers, slideshow, washi grain, aurora and embers landed on the web floors. Mobile has a wallpaper component that is not mounted.

22 Oct 2026Worlds without a sky

Six worlds dress the controls, but the hour only tints the ground by 0 to 12%.

21 to 26 Oct 2026Budget not joined

Verger can tell how much a phone can afford. It sits on its own branch and is not yet wired to the scene.

Feb vs Sep 2026What moves

North Star said everything has micro-motion; the later canon says surfaces stay still and only the companion moves.
