# EdLove and GuruDevi Editorial Publishing Vision

## Purpose

EdLove and GuruDevi should become two trustworthy publications with a shared
editorial foundation. The infrastructure should make it easy to publish useful
work, preserve author and source rights, support Sinhala and Tamil over time,
and distribute content to the places where readers already gather.

## The central idea

Use one editorial system but maintain two distinct publications.

EdLove is the broader intellectual and cultural home for education philosophy,
research, field notes, frameworks, and reflections on education as care work.
GuruDevi is the practical product and practice publication for teachers,
learners, families, schools, wellbeing, and platform updates.

The publications may link to and learn from one another, but neither should
silently duplicate the other's canonical work.

## Principles

- The website is the canonical source; social and third-party platforms are
  distribution channels.
- Every piece has an identifiable publication, author, source, language, and
  rights position.
- Human editorial judgment remains responsible for publication.
- Translation is a first-class editorial activity, not an afterthought.
- Contributors are welcomed through a safe, reviewable process.
- Automation assists publishing and distribution but does not bypass approval.
- Content should invite presence and understanding rather than manufacture
  attention.

## Intended reader journey

```text
Discover an idea
  -> Read the canonical article
  -> Follow related work
  -> Share or subscribe
  -> Join a conversation, practice, or product journey
```

The system should support this journey without turning the publications into a
generic social network or an engagement-optimization machine.

## Success

Success is not the number of posts. It is a growing body of durable, attributed,
accessible work that helps people understand education, care, and practice—and
helps the right readers find GuruDevi when it can genuinely serve them.
