# Gurudevi Soul & Core Covenant

> **One ecosystem, many doors. The lotus is the sanctuary; the firefly helps people move through it.**

This is the expanded governing definition of Gurudevi's product soul, core pillars, and ecosystem shape. Begin with [What Is Gurudevi?](what-is-gurudevi.md) for the short introduction, then use this Covenant for product decisions, client integrations, agentic capability, and implementation boundaries. It does not claim that every described capability is already shipped.

## 0. Naming and Scope

The terms in this Covenant have distinct scopes:

- **Gurudevi** names the educational ideal and the ecosystem built around it.
- **Gurudevi Sanctuary** names the integrated product environment: Self, Workspace, Library, Community, and Profile & Settings.
- **Gurudevi mobile app**, **Gurudevi web**, messaging channels, assistants, authorized agents, and paper/QR paths are different clients or doors into the Sanctuary.

No individual app, channel, or agent is the system of record or the whole of Gurudevi. Channels communicate; the Sanctuary's shared contracts, authority, and records endure across doors.

## 1. The Soul

Gurudevi exists to protect and cultivate the living relationship at the centre of education: teacher and student, learner and family, school and community. It treats people as whole participants in a shared life, never as rows in a surveillance CRM, engagement targets, or passive recipients of automation.

The name refers to the dignity and presence within the teaching-learning encounter. The software is its supporting substrate: a quiet sanctuary, a practical companion, and a set of tools that return time, clarity, and agency to people.

### The Soul in Practice

- **Relationship before extraction:** Learning and care outrank engagement metrics, advertising, profiling, and growth pressure.
- **Peace before feature count:** A workflow that creates panic, cognitive clutter, or coercion must be changed or refused.
- **Human authority before automation:** AI may understand, draft, and assist; people retain meaningful control over consequential actions.
- **Reality before fantasy:** The system respects actual devices, data costs, language, time, power, trust, and institutional practice.
- **Cultivation before performance:** Gurudevi is built patiently, with care for durable contracts and real users rather than launch theatre.

## 2. The Five Core Pillars

### I. Sanctuary — safety without extraction

Gurudevi is a protected digital ground: calm, private, failure-tolerant, and free from dark patterns. Core care and learning never depend on advertising, sponsor attention, or manipulative notification loops.

**Decision test:** Does this reduce unnecessary burden while protecting the person's attention, privacy, and dignity?

### II. Companion — never alone, never unsafe

The Firefly is a warm, restrained presence that helps people orient themselves, understand errors and offline states, and recover without shame. It is not a chattering mascot, a streak mechanic, or a substitute for human relationship.

**Decision test:** Does this make a person more capable and less stranded without demanding their attention?

### III. Convivial capability — bounded agency in real conditions

Powerhouse means practical human capability, not corporate control or feature bloat. Gurudevi offers tools that make people more able to act in their own circumstances: transparent calculations, local ownership, exportability, peer-to-peer exchange, and resilient offline work.

The system gives the best useful affordance available within real constraints. A voice note, a locally stored record, a paper/QR receipt, a web workspace, and an agent-prepared draft are different expressions of the same agency. None is a second-class path.

**Decision test:** Does this leave the person with more understandable control, including the ability to inspect, correct, export, and continue when a vendor or network fails?

### IV. Integrated ecosystem — the superapp re-imagined

Gurudevi is an integrated educational continuum for students, teachers, school leaders, families, and communities. The Sanctuary has four lived places—Self, Workspace, Library, and Community—plus Profile & Settings as the quiet edge for identity, privacy, memberships, and preferences.

Workspace takes the shape of the person's real work: **Learning Studio** for a student, **Classroom** for a teacher, **School** for a principal, and **Family Space** for a parent or guardian. These are role-shaped Workspace expressions, not competing top-level products.

"Superapp" does **not** mean a monolithic interface that replaces every familiar tool. It means one coherent ecosystem: shared identity, consent, policy, records, audit, and resilience across connected educational life.

**Decision test:** Does this connect life domains through a shared, understandable core while keeping each domain calm and bounded?

### V. Agentic intelligence with material realism

AI and automation serve as quiet caretakers: they remove clerical drudgery, draft work, explain, translate, and coordinate bounded tasks. They are additive, not a membership requirement.

The deterministic core remains usable without cloud intelligence. Attendance, grades, welfare, identity, fees, and other consequential records never depend on probabilistic output alone; an authorized person and deterministic rules verify the result.

**Decision test:** If the model, network, or automation runner is unavailable or wrong, can the person understand, recover, and continue safely?

## 3. The Sanctuary's Places and Doors

People should be able to meet Gurudevi in the place that best fits their moment and means. The interface is an affordance, not the product's identity.

```text
                         🌿 GURUDEVI SANCTUARY
                                      │
        ┌───────────────┬─────────────┼─────────────┬───────────────┐
        │               │             │             │               │
     🫶 Self       🛠️ Workspace   📚 Library   🤝 Community   👤 Profile
                         │
              ┌──────────┼──────────┬───────────┬────────────┐
              │          │          │           │            │
        🎓 Learning   👩‍🏫 Classroom  🏫 School  👨‍👩‍👧 Family Space
           Studio
```

### 🫶 Self — my personal place

Wellbeing, reflection, regulation, and personal growth. Self is for the person behind the role.

### 🛠️ Workspace — my real work

One place for educational work, expressed in language that makes sense to the person doing it:

| Person | Their Workspace |
| --- | --- |
| 🎓 Student | **Learning Studio** — learning work, assignments, progress, and classroom context |
| 👩‍🏫 Teacher | **Classroom** — teaching, attendance, lessons, learners, and evaluation |
| 🏫 Principal | **School** — leadership, school operations, people, and continuity |
| 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent or guardian | **Family Space** — linked learners, family-school connection, notices, and support |

### 📚 Library — knowledge and tools

One shared place for reading, materials, discovery, and useful tools. Its shelves adapt to the person: study resources for learners, teaching materials for educators, school references for leaders, and family-relevant learning resources for parents and guardians.

### 🤝 Community — people and shared life

One shared place for safe, purposeful connection. It may hold a learner's class connection, a teacher's staffroom, a principal's school community, or a parent’s family-school bridge—always with the right boundaries and visibility.

### 👤 Profile & Settings — my control edge

Identity, privacy, memberships, notifications, and preferences live here. Profile supports the Sanctuary; it is not another workroom.

### The ways people enter

The Sanctuary is one ecosystem with many optional doors:

| Door | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| 📱 Mobile app | everyday, on-the-go Sanctuary use |
| 🌐 Web | focused, larger-screen work |
| 💬 WhatsApp | familiar messages, voice notes, notices, and lightweight confirmations |
| ✈️ Telegram | community or conversational access where people already use it |
| ✉️ SMS | essential low-bandwidth notifications and recovery paths |
| 🎙️ Siri / Gemini / device assistants | hands-free read, draft, and confirmed actions on capable devices |
| 🤖 Authorized agents | bounded, audited assistance through approved capabilities |
| 🧾 Paper / QR | physical hand-offs, receipts, and bridges when they are more practical |

Each door may be optional, but the core must remain sovereign:

1. **Channels communicate; Gurudevi remembers and decides.** A messaging platform must not become the system of record.
2. **Clients express capabilities; they do not own domain rules.** The same authorized action should work through mobile, web, or a conversation adapter without duplicating business logic.
3. **Agents propose and execute bounded work; they do not inherit blanket authority.** Every tool call has an identified actor, scope, policy decision, expiry, and audit trail.
4. **The lowest viable path is a first-class path.** Essential work cannot require a new handset, constant open-data access, an installed app, or an AI assistant.
5. **Richer paths remain available.** A capable device, assistant, browser, or agent can remove extra effort, but it never gates care, learning, or belonging.

## 4. Progressive Affordance, Not a Single Imagined User

Gurudevi does not reduce people to a "rural" or "advanced" user persona. A teacher may have a modern phone but little time; a parent may prefer WhatsApp; a principal may need a large-screen Workspace; a family may share a device or have intermittent data.

The system should select, or let people select, the least-friction reliable path:

| Need | Essential expression | Enriched expression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Record a lesson or attendance | voice, local capture, later sync | assistant-created draft and confirmation card |
| Receive an essential notice | approved familiar channel or printable receipt | personalised in-app context and translation |
| Understand what to do next | simple guidance and clear recovery | conversational assistant, proactive summary, and automation |
| Work across school life | bounded domain room | connected cross-domain workspace |

The essential expression is the foundation, not a degraded fallback. The enriched expression is an additive lift.

## 5. Capability and Authority Covenant

Every interaction—button tap, voice note, message, browser action, or assistant request—must resolve to a shared capability under policy. The core decides whether it can answer, create a draft, request clarification, require approval, execute a deterministic command, or refuse.

| Capability class | Examples | Authority rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read | today's classes, absence summary, policy explanation | authorized view; disclose the minimum necessary data |
| Draft | a parent notice, lesson record, translation, reconciliation suggestion | clearly marked as a draft; no silent domain mutation |
| Confirmed command | record verified attendance, send an approved utility notice | named authorized actor confirms material effect |
| Restricted command | grades, welfare, fees, identity, exports, deletion | role-specific policy, stronger confirmation, full audit; never delegated broadly |

This covenant applies equally to internal UI, WhatsApp, Telegram, Siri or Gemini integrations, MCP clients, WebMCP tools, and workflow runners. MCP is an agent interoperability interface; n8n and agent frameworks are replaceable orchestration mechanisms. None is a source of truth.

## 6. Architectural Non-Negotiables

1. **Local-first and recoverable:** Critical work persists locally or has an explicit durable recovery path before relying on the cloud.
2. **Deterministic records:** Core educational, legal, financial, and identity invariants are enforced by deterministic domain logic.
3. **Consent and minimum disclosure:** Channel integrations receive only the information necessary for the selected, authorized interaction.
4. **Human review at consequential seams:** Automation drafts; an accountable person confirms material changes.
5. **Portable, auditable agency:** People and institutions can inspect, correct, export, and migrate their records. Every consequential action is attributable.
6. **Graceful degradation:** Network, device, model, or vendor failure must reduce convenience—not erase essential agency.
7. **No silent capability fiction:** Product documents distinguish implemented behaviour, committed work, and long-range direction.

## 7. Maturity Direction

The order matters. New channels and agents must be attached to a trustworthy core, not used to substitute for one.

1. Establish shared capability, consent, policy, audit, and durable-command contracts.
2. Prove one end-to-end, human-confirmed workflow through one existing channel.
3. Reuse the same contract from web and mobile clients.
4. Add further messaging and assistant adapters only when they preserve the covenant.
5. Expose narrowly scoped, auditable capabilities to external agents through MCP or successor standards.

## 8. Decision Questions

Before adding a feature, client, integration, or agent tool, ask:

1. Which pillar does this serve, and what burden does it remove?
2. What is the least-friction path for the person in their actual situation?
3. Can they still complete the essential work if this client, network, or model fails?
4. Who has authority to confirm or reverse the material outcome?
5. Does the change strengthen the shared ecosystem, or create another disconnected island?
