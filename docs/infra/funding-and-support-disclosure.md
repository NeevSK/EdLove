# Gurudevi Funding & Support Disclosure

This document is the public-facing scaffold for support. It is a disclosure
format, not an offer, payment instruction, or promise that funding is currently
accepted.

## Accepted support in principle

Gurudevi may consider grants, gifts, aligned institutional income, paid
contracts, infrastructure credits, and bounded partnerships. Each material
relationship is reviewed against the [Open Stewardship covenant](../decisions/2026-10-11-open-stewardship-and-aligned-support-decision.md).

## Terms that are not for sale

Support does not purchase student or teacher data, surveillance, advertising
placement outside the Energy Reservoir, editorial control, governance control,
exclusivity, a roadmap veto, or a requirement to make core care dependent on
payment or sponsor-media viewing.

## Disclosure record template

For each material relationship, publish a record with:

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Supporter | Legal or public name, unless safety requires an anonymous entry |
| Form | Grant, gift, contract, credit, partnership, or other |
| Date and period | Acceptance date and commitment period |
| Amount or value | Currency/value, or “not disclosed for safety/legal reasons” |
| Intended use | Infrastructure, accessibility, research, operations, or named work |
| Constraints | All material conditions, reporting, or visibility expectations |
| Conflicts | Any relationship that could affect product direction |
| Decision | Accepted, declined, or accepted with safeguards |

## Energy Reservoir disclosure

The Energy Reservoir is a separate opt-in attention-for-virtual-capacity path.
It is the only permitted sponsor-media location. It cannot appear in groups,
direct messages, feeds, notifications, Classroom, Self, Library, Community,
Household, or essential workflows. It cannot be used to purchase data or
governance influence.

The implementation remains stop-shipped until the security gates in the
[Rewarded Ad Center policy](../decisions/2026-08-31-rewarded-ad-center-policy-and-compliance.md)
are evidenced.
