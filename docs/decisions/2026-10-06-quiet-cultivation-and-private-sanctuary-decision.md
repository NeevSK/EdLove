# Quiet Cultivation & Private Sanctuary Decision

> **Superseded on 2026-10-11.** This decision remains as the record of why
> performative launch pressure, public fundraising machinery, and sponsor-driven
> product capture are unacceptable. The current policy is
> [Open Stewardship & Aligned Support](2026-10-11-open-stewardship-and-aligned-support-decision.md):
> Gurudevi may open its work and accept aligned support without accepting those
> harms.

## Founder Statement & Context

During early planning, a significant amount of energy was spent preparing for public open-source distribution, public sponsorship campaigns, institutional fundraising tiers, and formal launch checklists. 

However, this exploratory energy introduced an invisible, crushing emotional and operational tax:
1. The performance anxiety of external expectations, public scrutiny, and internet-maintainer burnout.
2. The risk of having to constantly justify and dilute deeply cultural, sacred, and Sri Lankan educational nuances to generic tech audiences.
3. The cognitive fatigue of managing public governance, sponsor tiers, and commercial/crowdfunding apparatus instead of focusing on the actual software and the real human beings it serves.

Inspired by Jean Giono and Frédéric Back’s *The Man Who Planted Trees* (1987), this platform embraces the peaceful, unhurried path of Elzéard Bouffier: **planting seeds in complete silence and devotion, without demanding an audience, without performing for the internet, and without carrying the noise of the public sphere.**

## The Decision

1. **Private, Sovereign Sanctuary by Default**:
   - Gurudevi and EdLove will **not** pursue an aggressive public marketing launch, public crowdfunding campaign, or open-source community circus.
   - The repository and platform remain a private, protected workshop and intimate sanctuary dedicated solely to real classrooms, teachers, and students.

2. **No Public Commercial/Sponsorship Overhead**:
   - All public donation tiers, fundraising evolution experiments, sponsor badges, and public launch checklists are decommissioned from active development roots and placed into long-term archive.
   - Human connection remains completely free, direct, and unmonetized. Anyone with goodwill or curiosity can speak to the founder without transaction or barrier.

3. **Focus on Direct Reality (The Quiet Forest)**:
   - Engineering effort is redirected 100% toward core software craftsmanship: offline-first resilience, local privacy, intuitive user experience, and genuine school utility.
