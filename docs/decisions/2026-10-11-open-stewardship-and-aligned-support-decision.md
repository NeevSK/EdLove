# Open Stewardship & Aligned Support Decision

## Decision

Gurudevi will prepare for a public repository and may accept aligned grants,
gifts, institutional income, partnerships, and other support. Openness is a
practice of accountable stewardship, not a transfer of the platform's purpose,
user relationships, or ethical direction to funders, advertisers, or the
loudest external voices.

## Transparent by Default, Safe by Design

The project will disclose its purpose, decision records, roadmap rationale,
material funding sources, material terms, and use of support. It will not
disclose credentials, security-sensitive operational detail, minors' or users'
data, private correspondence, or personal information whose publication would
make people or the Sanctuary less safe.

## Aligned Support Covenant

Outside critique and support may nudge product direction through evidence,
school experience, and respectful dialogue. They may not compel changes that
violate the following non-negotiables:

1. Human dignity, privacy, local-first resilience, equitable core access, and
   the Sanctuary's calm are protected.
2. Support does not buy user data, advertising access outside the Energy
   Reservoir, editorial control, governance control, exclusivity, or a veto over
   the platform's core direction.
3. No agreement may make essential care, reflection, learning, records, or
   human connection contingent on payment or sponsor-media viewing.
4. No funder, advertiser, or partner may require dark patterns, behavioural
   surveillance, public performance obligations, or unnecessary urgency.
5. Material support relationships are written down before acceptance and
   disclosed at a level consistent with safety and law.

## The Energy Reservoir

The optional Rewarded Ad Center is a bounded attention-for-virtual-capacity
exchange. It is the sole permitted sponsor-media surface and is not an
advertising exception for the rest of the product. Its additional requirements
are recorded in the Rewarded Ad Center policy; until those technical safeguards
are proven in production-like evidence, it remains unavailable as a live revenue
mechanism.

## What This Does Not Authorize

This decision does not publish a repository, connect a funding provider, accept
money, reveal protected information, enable ads, or weaken any security,
privacy, child-safety, or release gate. Those are separate, accountable actions
that require their own evidence and operational decision.

## Relationship to the Prior Decision

The 2026-10-06 private-sanctuary decision correctly identified the harms of
performative public launch and sponsorship machinery. This decision preserves
that insight while replacing withdrawal with bounded openness and aligned
support.
