# Gurudevi Core Definition: The Convivial Sanctuary & Integrated Ecosystem

> **Superseded:** The current canonical definition is [Gurudevi Soul & Core Covenant](../Core/gurudevi-soul-and-core-covenant.md). This decision record is retained as historical context.

## Context & Synthesis

On 2026-10-10, founder Naveen and Antigravity held an architectural and philosophical review to redefine and codify the core identity of Gurudevi. 

This covenant reconciles the peace of the **Private Sanctuary** ([2026-10-06](file:///c:/x/docs/decisions/2026-10-06-quiet-cultivation-and-private-sanctuary-decision.md)) with the transformative ambition of a **Powerhouse Ecosystem**, grounding both in the material reality of Sri Lankan education and the ethics of **Convivial Tools**.

---

## 1. The Five Core Pillars

### I. Sanctuary (Safe, Unhurried, Zero Extraction)
* **Definition:** A protected digital ground that does not rush, exploit, track, or extract from the human spirit.
* **The Guarantee:** Zero ad-bloat, zero cognitive assault, and zero dark
  patterns. Sponsor media is prohibited throughout the Sanctuary and its core
  workflows. The only permitted exception is the separate, user-opened Energy
  Reservoir governed by the rewarded-attention policy; it is voluntary,
  bounded, and never a condition of core care or learning.
* **The Pruning Rule:** If any feature, workflow, or metric creates cognitive burden, clutter, or panic for the teacher or learner, **it can and must be removed.** Peace and human agency always outrank feature count.

### II. The Companion (The Firefly / "Never Alone, Never Unsafe")
* **Definition:** A warm, luminous guide that accompanies the human through the software.
* **The Role:** *"The lotus is the sanctuary; the firefly helps you move through it."*
* **Behavior:** It softens error states, guides orientation, clarifies offline/sync transitions, and removes the intimidation of complex tools. It is not an intrusive, chattering mascot; it is a quiet presence ensuring the user never feels stranded or abandoned.

### III. Powerhouse through Convivial Tools
* **Definition:** Powerhouse does not mean corporate domination or feature bloat; it means **autonomous human capability** (Ivan Illich's *Tools for Conviviality*).
* **The Reality:** Giving teachers, students, and communities real leverage to confront their everyday context and material problems.
* **Affordances:** Peer-to-peer data sharing (Bluetooth/mesh/direct export), local data sovereignty, transparent calculations, and tools that enhance human competence rather than fostering platform dependency.

### IV. The Integrated Ecosystem ("Superapp" Re-imagined)
* **Definition:** Not a siloed single-player study app, and not a detached administrative database, but the **entire educational continuum** harmonized:
  - **Students:** Learning, inquiry, reading, and personal growth.
  - **Teachers:** Daily pedagogical craft, attendance, evaluation, and reflective inner life.
  - **Administrators:** Regulatory compliance, Gazette circulars, and institutional operations.
  - **Families & Households:** 13-year student records, sibling derivations, and statutory welfare (Aswesuma).
* **Device Harmony:** Complex administrative workflows gracefully adapt to larger screens (Web/Tablet), but mobile provides the maximum possible operational affordance without crushing the mobile viewport.

### V. Agentic Intelligence & Material Realism
* **Definition:** Technology serving human labor through autonomous task execution, while respecting physical laws.
* **The Material Invariant:** Physical reality, battery limits, weak hardware, and intermittent connectivity do not bow to moral wishes. Therefore:
  - **Democratic Access:** High-tier AI capabilities are bridged to the maximum extent possible across the entire community, not hoarded solely behind paywalls.
  - **Agentic Delegation:** Background workflows automate heavy, repetitive bureaucratic burdens (grading logs, record reconciliation, drafting, scheduling).
  - **Graceful Capability Fallback:** AI and agentic execution scale gracefully to the physical substrate. The deterministic core is 100% offline-functional; cloud agentic inference is an additive lift when network physics allow.

## 2. The Architectural Covenant (Engine Rules)

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      GURUDEVI ARCHITECTURE                       │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  SURFACE:      Sanctuary Shell (Quiet, Unhurried, Restrained)   │
│  PRESENCE:     Firefly Companion (Safe, Attentive, Guiding)      │
│  SANCTUARY:    🫶 Self | 🛠️ Workspace | 📚 Library | 🤝 Community  │
│  WORKSPACE:    🎓 Learning Studio | 👩‍🏫 Classroom | 🏫 School | 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Space │
│  CONTROL EDGE: 👤 Profile & Settings                            │
│  CAPABILITIES: Convivial Tools (P2P, Local Power, Automation)    │
│  SUBSTRATE:    Offline-First Engine (SQLite, PowerSync, MMKV)    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Local-First Is Non-Negotiable:** Gurudevi assumes the network is down and power is unstable (the rural Sri Lankan reality). All core data operations persist locally first.
2. **Domain-Floor Isolation:** Operational depth stays inside bounded domain floors ([Canvas Model Decision](file:///c:/x/docs/decisions/2026-09-14-gurudevi-canvas-model-and-domain-floor-authority.md)). Feature furniture never spills out to create a chaotic home screen.
3. **Deterministic Core vs. Probabilistic AI:** No core domain invariant (attendance, grades, legal welfare, sibling links) may depend on non-deterministic LLM output. AI assists and drafts; the deterministic engine and the human verify.
