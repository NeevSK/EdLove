## After P4 (thermal and storage hysteresis)

Heat now rises at once and steps down only past an exit band (headroom 0.05, battery 1 °C) held
for a dwell (20 s); lowering acts on the reading itself, relaxing waits on the held level. The
metrics gained `chatter`: heat-level changes the true temperature does not explain (it moved less
than 0.5 °C since the previous change), and the first reading no longer counts as a change.

Edge sessions (a steady load parked on each phone's first heat threshold, 20 minutes), heat
changes / chatter per minute, p4 and p5 alike, before and after P4:

| phone | seed | before | after |
|---|---|---|---|
| low (battery temperature only) | 1 | 0.35 / 0.30 | 0.05 / 0.00 |
| low | 2 | 0.55 / 0.50 | 0.05 / 0.00 |
| low | 3 | 0.35 / 0.30 | 0.05 / 0.00 |
| mid | 1 | 0.10 / 0.05 | 0.10 / 0.05 |
| mid | 3 | 0.20 / 0.15 | 0.20 / 0.15 |
| high | 1–3 | 0.05 / 0.00 | 0.05 / 0.00 |

The mid phone's remaining chatter is the model's OS status: discrete, with no hysteresis of its
own, flipping every 30–40 s at exactly 40 °C, past the dwell. Real thermal HALs add their own
hysteresis there, so this is the pessimistic case. The cost of the dwell: settling after a heat
soak is 15–35 s longer (low 64 → 110 s, mid 76 → 92 s, high 36 → 52 s for p4); quality given up
in the mid burst rose 25 → 30% (warm floors held through the dwell), which P8 addresses.
