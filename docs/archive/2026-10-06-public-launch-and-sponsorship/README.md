## Context

These files were created during earlier phases when public open-source launch, public sponsorships (GitHub Sponsors, Ko-fi, Wise tiers), and institutional launch readiness checklists were being drafted.

On October 6, 2026, the founder made the definitive decision to step away from public launch performance, external fundraising burdens, and the psychological overhead of public maintenance. 

Embracing the ethos of *The Man Who Planted Trees* (1987), Gurudevi / EdLove continues as a private, intimate, sovereign craft—quietly planting seeds for real schools and teachers without public performance or commercial noise.
