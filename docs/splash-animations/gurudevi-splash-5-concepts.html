Gurudevi Splash — Five Directions (v4)

1 — Sunrise

The heart sparks first; a circle of light expands and reveals the mark from within.

2 — Two Paths Crossing

Teacher and student sweep in as trailing comets, meet in a ripple, settle, and the bond rises before light drifts from the heart.

3 — Ink Bloom (fixed: real morph)

The ink itself IS the petal now — each blob's own outline morphs directly into the precise petal shape. One continuous form, no swap.

4 — Line Draw (reworked)

Starts at the bottom, loops back to bottom, then heart lines draw simultaneously. Once the lines finish, one unified circle of light reveals all three petals' color together, expanding outward from the heart — no per-petal fades to flicker against the linework.

5 — Living Breath

No build — the mark is simply there, and breathes slowly on a loop.
