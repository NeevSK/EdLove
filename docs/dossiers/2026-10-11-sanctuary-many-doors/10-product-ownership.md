# Product Ownership: Keep the Sky Open and Earn Each Release

## Possibility and product identity

The product is a Sanctuary for educational life whose interface can change. Its four lived places remain simple while capabilities deepen. A learner can grow into a teacher or parent without fragmented identities; their institutional relationships change while personal agency continues. The long-term promise is humane continuity and useful affordance, not ownership of everyone's day.

The superapp vision remains broad. Product cohesion comes from understandable places, compatible records, shared rights, and continuity. A WhatsApp interaction may express one small capability without displaying the whole Sanctuary. A rich web Workspace may expose more. Both can belong to the same product.

## Portfolios, not one giant release train

Maintain five concurrent possibility portfolios: personal Sanctuary, educational work, knowledge/community, access channels/assistants, and institutional continuity/portability. Each can have discoveries and experiments without becoming a release promise. Private local tools can mature while school records are hardened. Library accessibility does not need to wait for external MCP. Federation can be explored with a bounded export/import experiment.

Portfolio growth still competes for one team's maintenance capacity. Allocate experiment time explicitly. Publishing a future page does not create permission to let people rely on an incomplete effect.

## Choose the first proof

Candidate A: a teacher creates and corrects a private lesson draft, then explicitly saves it as an authorized classroom record. Candidate B: a school sends one approved family notice with linked recipient/consent and durable delivery status. Candidate C: useful offline Library access with recovery and provenance. Choose after observing the task, institutional acceptance, and operating cost.

A is initially attractive because it can start with text/manual entry and add voice later. B has useful familiar-channel value but greater linkage/disclosure/delivery obligations. C can prove continuity with less consequential mutation but does not prove the future command boundary. Keep those distinctions; there is no universal best pilot.

For a command pilot, first prove structured input and domain commit. Add the channel adapter and optional AI extraction to that proven lifecycle. Offer manual input whenever inference is missing or wrong. The action must remain usable when AI is disabled.

## Definition of delivered

Delivery includes useful output, role/scope correctness, accurate status, recovery after interrupted work, participant comprehension, correction/export, and a named support owner. Every action gets a platform and maturity label: conceptual, demo, contract verified, runtime verified, or operationally observed. The existing capability manifest is a starting point, not proof of cross-channel parity.

Recommended evidence ladder: G0 contracts/static checks; G1 boundary assertions; G2 defined multi-step scenarios; real owned-seam/database evidence; G3 mounted component; G4 browser/Metro journey; G5 physical-device path; R1 bounded pilot/operational observation. Apply repository evidence rules to the relevant surface; do not rename a mocked scenario as physical or production proof.

## Prioritization and stop conditions

Prioritize by participant value, harmful-failure potential, learning gained, implementation seam reuse, and total cost. Avoid numerical scoring that pretends subjective weights are objective truth.

Stop or reshape an experiment when net burden rises, drafts invent facts, essential users cannot recover without staff, sensitive linkage is ambiguous, operators cannot reconcile records, costs exceed the supported envelope, or a sponsor changes the purpose of care. A local tool may be a successful outcome even if a wider automation idea fails.

## Roadmap accountability

The windows in [11](11-integration-scenarios-and-milestones.md) are conditional scenarios. Staffing, budgets, institutional access, independent review, and production inventory are unknown. Gates determine progress; elapsed time does not authorize skipping them. Larger-scale phases require new evidence rather than extrapolating a pilot's enthusiasm.

Assign role ownership before implementation: product steward for purpose/release truth; domain steward for record meaning; engineer for lifecycle; security owner for identity/privacy; operator for incidents/restore; institution contact for disputes; finance steward for recurring sustainability. One person may initially hold several roles, but a role table is not redundancy. Document replacement and absence coverage.

## Integration round R9

Adopt dream-first exploration and evidence-gated execution. Preserve the full horizon while selecting one useful proof. Consolidate R1–R8 into capability-specific readiness rather than a “platform complete” claim. The next implementation scope must identify exact existing contracts/services, participant task, authoritative record, permitted channels, failure states, owner, and evidence. This dossier supports that choice; it does not authorize an unbounded rewrite.
