# Business Analysis: Adoption, Institutional Work, and Value

## Possibility

Gurudevi can provide shared educational capability across public schools, tuition settings, families, and independent learners. Value may emerge through cooperative stewardship, institutional service, supported infrastructure, or public-interest partnerships. The product can be valuable without capturing all communication or monetizing every participant.

The payer, beneficiary, administrator, record custodian, and daily user may be different people. Each has different incentives. A principal's reporting benefit can become a teacher's data-entry cost. Family access can become intrusive if a school treats participation as compulsory. These tensions belong in requirements, not a later marketing discussion.

## Map actual work before automating it

For a candidate workflow, identify trigger, owner, source, required record, existing WhatsApp/paper/app steps, correction, sign-off, retention, and recipient. Determine whether the record is informal assistance or an institutionally accepted artifact. A generated “lesson record” is not automatically accepted in the format or process the teacher must follow.

| Candidate | Potential value | Cost transferred to someone else | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Teacher lesson draft | Less rewriting after class | Checking a poor transcript or wrong template | Time saved including correction; accepted output format |
| Family notice | Familiar essential communication | Linking children, managing outdated numbers, reply handling | Consent, ownership, understandable receipt and dispute path |
| Attendance capture | Less repeated copying | Reconciling paper and digital records | Authoritative owner and correction policy |
| Principal summary | Faster operational awareness | Pressure on staff to over-report | Completeness limits and non-surveillance boundaries |
| Library downloads | Usable learning resources | Storage/data cost and copyright administration | Device fit, provenance, redistribution rights |
| Personal reflection | Private support and self-directed learning | Trust risk if school visibility is ambiguous | Clear personal ownership and privacy comprehension |

## Entry strategies

One institution can pilot a coherent workflow with a named owner and existing practice retained during validation. Individuals can adopt private tools or Library independently. A school-wide rollout may later connect these paths, but should not require every parent to create a modern app account immediately.

Channel integrations can act as bridges: a notice gives useful information in chat and offers optional deeper context in the Sanctuary. A bot should earn repeated use through reliable value. Do not assume people will move a class group, change phone habits, or grant new permissions because the ecosystem is elegant.

Onboarding debt is operational debt: wrong learner linkage, departed teachers, recycled numbers, unclaimed notices, duplicate institutions, and ambiguous roles generate support cases. Measure these alongside feature completion. A small institution may require more support per participant than a large technically capable school.

## Institutional governance

Assign record custody, role issuance/revocation, disputes, corrections, support escalation, and export/exit. School changes and multiple roles require explicit relationships; a parent-teacher can hold both without access bleeding across contexts. Establish who authorizes a school to join and who acts if leadership changes.

Current local law, child-data requirements, procurement rules, retention duties, and contractual terms require qualified review before live institutional operation. No legal compliance claim is made here. Maintain a dated obligations register with owner and evidence rather than importing generic consent text.

## Value and adoption tests

Net burden reduction = existing task effort minus new task effort minus correction, parallel entry, support, training, and reporting overhead. Observe a baseline, then compare with assisted completion under ordinary and interrupted conditions. A positive result for one enthusiastic teacher does not establish school-wide adoption.

Possible measures: correct task completion, avoided duplicate work, recoverable errors, parent comprehension, reduced follow-up calls, support minutes per institution, and voluntary continued use. Avoid engagement/streaks as proxies for educational benefit. Principals and funders should not convert these measures into a ranking of children or teachers.

## Hostile business cases

An institution asks for automated teacher performance rankings. This conflicts with the Sanctuary even if revenue is attractive. A sponsor requires advertising in essential family notices. This changes who the channel serves. A pilot needs a second full-time clerk to repair drafts. Apparent automation has displaced work rather than removed it. A school cannot maintain either a hub or its credentials. Federation is then an operating liability, not sovereignty.

## Integration round R4

Carry forward one owned, institutionally meaningful task with measured net burden. Preserve individual adoption and optional channels. UX success must include the whole workflow; finance must price onboarding and support; security must map custody/revocation. A school pilot advances only when owners understand correction, continuity, and exit, with no compulsory private-life disclosure.
