# Gurudevi and EdLove Content Ownership and Publishing

## Ownership boundary

One Function of EdLove is the canonical home for philosophical work, research, essays, frameworks, and long-form intellectual property. Gurudevi owns product explanation, user guidance, platform documentation, and role-based experience copy.

Gurudevi may summarize or link to EdLove work, but should not silently duplicate or fork canonical research.

## Publishing model

EdLove content remains Git-versioned Markdown/MDX with typed frontmatter. The publishing surface should support `Article`, `Research`, `Essay`, and `Field Note` types, author/source metadata, publication and update dates, tags, related work, canonical URLs, licenses, RSS/Atom, sitemaps, and accessible long-form reading layouts.

Initial categories include teaning, Sri Lankan education research, teacher dignity, education ecology, Gurudevi philosophy, and transparency notes.

## Attribution

Any Gurudevi summary of EdLove work must identify EdLove as the source, link to the canonical article, preserve author and license information, and distinguish summary from original research.
