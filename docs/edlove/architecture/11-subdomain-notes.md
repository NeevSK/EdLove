## 🧭 CANONICAL ROUTING & ALIAS STRATEGY

**Strategic Realities:**
1. **Product Autonomy with Collaborative Roots**: Gurudevi operates as its own dedicated product at `gurudevi.app` and `sanctuary.gurudevi.app` while proudly maintaining its co-creation heritage with EdLove.
2. **National Localized Gateways**: Both `gurudevi.lk` and `intuituilabs.lk` provide trusted national accessibility within Sri Lanka.
3. **Permanent 301 Redirects**: Any legacy traffic to `*.edlove.org` satellite hosts is routed smoothly to their respective canonical homes without link rot or SEO loss.
