## The three arms

Each arm inherits EdLove's vision, philosophy and theories.

### EdResearch: research

EdResearch works at two orders: research on education (first order), and research on education research itself (second order, meta-research). EdLove holds the intent; technical and analytical support comes, so far, from Intuitui Labs.

1. Teaning (TSR, the teacher–student relation)
2. System view
3. Fate of EdTech solutions
4. Meta-research on education research in Sri Lanka
5. Critical realist analysis of the schooling system

### EdCare: outward giving

EdCare is the arm that gives outward. It holds two modes, separated by one criterion: EdKind changes what the recipient *has*; EdLife changes what the recipient *can do*.

- **EdKind (material support**): charity, connecting people, distributing material. Inexpensive, easy transfers.
- **EdLife**: human development — teaching and grooming people in as helpful a manner as possible. Developmental work, not charity.

Both modes are open; no work has started yet.

### EdTech: technological solutions

**Gurudevi** ([gurudevi.app](https://gurudevi.app)) is EdTech's instance, holding three roles:

1. A design science intervention
2. An ideological vehicle
3. A research artefact, feeding feedback back into EdResearch

The third role closes the loop: research informs Gurudevi, and Gurudevi returns evidence to research.

## Intuitui Labs

Intuitui Labs is a technology firm for any domain, separate from the EdLove Foundation. It stands to EdLove in two relations:

- **Builder of Gurudevi**, its first project.
- **Technical and analytical support for EdResearch.**

Canonical phrasing for every site: *Gurudevi, an EdLove initiative, built by Intuitui Labs.*

Boundary: EdLove holds the intent of all research; Intuitui Labs supplies the technicalities, analytics and other support, as it has so far.

## Naming the material mode

**EdKind** is the name of EdCare's material mode, settled on 22 November 2026.

*In kind* is the established term for non-cash giving: goods, supplies, food, equipment. *Kind* also carries kindness; both senses grow from Old English *gecynd* and *gecynde*, "nature, origin, kin". EdKind gives in kind; EdLife gives in capacity.

Boundary: in-kind giving can include services. Services that teach belong to EdLife; goods and their logistics belong to EdKind.

Candidates considered and set aside:

| Name | Reason set aside |
| --- | --- |
| EdDana | Religious connotation for some readers |
| EdShare | Generic; little character |
| EdFlow | Abstract to a donor or volunteer |
| EdBridge | Underplays material distribution |
| EdReach | Overlaps with EdLife's outward work |
| Ripple | Collides with the Ripple crypto brand; no Ed- prefix |
| Drizzle | Can read as meagre; collides with Drizzle ORM |
| EdIble | Reads as food; cannabis connotation; light tone |
| EdFuel | Theoretically apt but corporate in tone |
| EdHand | Says little about what is given |
| EduKind | Breaks the Ed- family pattern |

The family now reads as one: EdResearch, EdCare (EdKind and EdLife), EdTech.
