## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

**Schoolhouse.world** is a global non-profit peer-tutoring and **authentic competency-certification platform** launched by Sal Khan during the 2020 global school closures to make free, high-trust, small-group live tutoring accessible to any student on earth regardless of family income.

Commercial online tutoring platforms charge $40–$100 per hour, locking lower-income students out while high-stakes standardized tests (SAT, AP exams) exacerbate global socioeconomic inequality. Schoolhouse.world revolutionized two key mechanisms:
- **Free Volunteer Peer-to-Peer Small Group Pods**: High school and college students who master a subject (e.g. Calculus, SAT Math, Physics) host free, live, 4-to-6 student interactive video tutoring sessions.
- **Empirical Video Mastery Certification**: Replaces expensive standardized testing fees with a **recorded video defense**: students take a randomized Khan Academy mastery test while screen-recording themselves and explaining their step-by-step reasoning out loud. Certified human peer reviewers audit the video, awarding a cryptographically verifiable **Mastery Transcript**.
- **Official Elite University Admissions Recognition**: Prestigious universities (MIT, Yale, Caltech, Chicago) accept Schoolhouse.world Mastery Transcripts directly into their official undergraduate admissions portfolios as rigorous proof of subject mastery and civic altruism.

## 2. Architectural & Technical Foundation

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ SCHOOLHOUSE.WORLD PEER CERTIFICATION TOPOLOGY                          │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Video Engine: Zoom Web SDK / Embedded WebRTC interactive video rooms │
│ • Certification Pipeline: Screen + Webcam Video Submission Harness     │
│ • Peer Audit Queue: Multi-reviewer double-blind video verification     │
│ • Portfolio Generator: Cryptographically signed public Mastery URL     │
│ • Safety Subsystem: AI transcription, audio keyword safety monitoring  │
│ • Subsidized Cost: 100% Free; funded by philanthropic foundations      │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **The Video-Recorded Mastery Verification Protocol**:
   - A student solves Khan Academy unit test problems live with webcam enabled. They explain their algebraic reasoning out loud in real-time, proving authentic conceptual grasp and eliminating cheating or AI substitution.

2. **The Double-Blind Peer Review Audit Queue**:
   - Video submissions are routed to certified peer and adult auditors who check for conceptual clarity, timing, and reasoning integrity. Approvals issue an immutable **Portfolio Certificate** containing the verified video snippet.

3. **Automated Child Safety & Safeguarding Shield**:
   - All live Zoom sessions are recorded, transcribed via automated Speech-to-Text, and scanned by AI safety monitors for inappropriate language, bullying, or privacy violations, protecting minors without requiring constant adult surveillance in every breakout room.

## 4. Synthesis & Key Lessons for EdLove & Gurudevi (Sri Lanka)

### Strengths to Emulate:
1. **The Video Defense as Living Credential**: Proves that a 5-minute video of a student explaining their thinking is a far richer, more honest assessment of intellect than an ambiguous multiple-choice grade.
2. **The Virtuous Circle of Tutoring**: High school students gain profound confidence and leadership skills by tutoring younger students, living out *Docendo Discimus*.

### Limitations:
1. **Live High-Speed Video Bandwidth**: Streaming live multi-party Zoom video requires stable broadband connections, which can strain rural cellular connections in developing nations.

### ✦ Direct Grounding for Sri Lankan Peer Mentorship Networks:
- Sri Lankan students who achieve top island ranks (Island 1st–100th) in the G.C.E. Advanced Level (A/L) exams are revered as national heroes, but their insights rarely reach struggling rural students.
- EdLove can implement a **localized Schoolhouse.world peer-mentorship and video-defense protocol**: connecting top Sri Lankan university scholars with rural O/L and A/L students for free live study pods, issuing verified *EdLove Mentorship Badges* that validate their pedagogical service.
