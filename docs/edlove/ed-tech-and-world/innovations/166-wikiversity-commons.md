## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

**Wikiversity Open Learning Community** represents a significant milestone within **14. Open Educational Resources & Open Access (13 Entries)**.

### Core Tenet:
Wikimedia project dedicated to learning resources, study projects, and collaborative research for all age groups.

Historically, traditional educational institutions and digital systems have struggled with centralized extraction, administrative fatigue, or network fragility. Wikiversity Open Learning Community addresses this by reimagining how educational interactions are mediated and sustained.
