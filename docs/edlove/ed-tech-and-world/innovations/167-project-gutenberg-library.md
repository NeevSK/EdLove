## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

**Project Gutenberg Free Ebooks** represents a significant milestone within **14. Open Educational Resources & Open Access (13 Entries)**.

### Core Tenet:
The world's oldest digital library of over 70,000 free public domain cultural and literary ebooks.

Historically, traditional educational institutions and digital systems have struggled with centralized extraction, administrative fatigue, or network fragility. Project Gutenberg Free Ebooks addresses this by reimagining how educational interactions are mediated and sustained.
