> **Category**: 6. National & Provincial Digital Leaps

> **License**: 100% Free Philanthropic Educational Commons

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

**DP Education**, founded in 2019 by Sri Lankan business magnate and philanthropist Dhammika Perera, is the largest, most pervasive, and most successful **free, trilingual, digital education broadcast and IT infrastructure initiative in the history of Sri Lanka**.

In Sri Lanka, the public education system has long been undermined by regional resource disparities: rural schools in Monaragala or Kilinochchi lack qualified teachers for advanced mathematics, science, and English, forcing parents into crippling debt to pay for private commercial tuition classes. DP Education created a **national digital equalizing bridge**:
- **100% Free Complete National Curriculum (Grades 1 to 13)**: Professionally recorded, studio-quality video lessons covering the entire National Institute of Education (NIE) syllabus in **Sinhala, Tamil, and English** with top-tier national educators.
- **DP Education IT Campus (In Partnership with University of Moratuwa)**: Free, full-stack software development courses (Python, JavaScript, Full-Stack Web Development, Cloud Computing) mapped to official University of Moratuwa certifications.
- **Physical Village Coding Centers**: Establishing free physical computer labs with high-speed internet in rural Buddhist temples and community centres to give village children direct hands-on access to PCs.

1. **YouTube-First Scalable Broadcast Architecture**:
   - By leveraging YouTube’s global CDN infrastructure as its primary streaming backbone, DP Education bypassed expensive custom video hosting costs, delivering instant, 1080p, zero-lag streaming to millions of Sri Lankan homes simultaneously during COVID-19 lockdowns.

2. **University of Moratuwa Enterprise Certification**:
   - Partnered with Sri Lanka’s premier engineering faculty (University of Moratuwa) to co-design the "Trainee Full Stack Developer" program—enabling any rural school leaver to earn an official university-certified software credential for free.

3. **Zero-Rated Mobile Data Integration**:
   - Negotiated zero-rated data access agreements with major Sri Lankan telecommunication providers (Dialog Axiata, SLT-Mobitel), allowing impoverished families with zero cellular data balance to stream educational content without cost.

## 4. Synthesis & Key Lessons for EdLove & Gurudevi (Sri Lanka)

### Strengths to Emulate:
1. **Uncompromising Trilingual Parity**: Elevating the Tamil and Sinhala educational corpus to equal studio excellence, fostering national reconciliation and equity.
2. **Pragmatic Physical Infrastructure**: Recognizing that digital content is useless if rural kids have no physical computers—hence building physical IT labs in village temples.

### Limitations:
1. **One-Way Broadcast Pedagogy**: Video broadcasting remains an asymmetric transmission medium—lacking real-time Socratic dialogue, peer feedback, or active cognitive reflection.

### ✦ Direct Synergy with EdLove & Gurudevi:
- DP Education provides the **massive broadcast library of national syllabus lectures** that Sri Lanka desperately needed.
- **EdLove and Gurudevi provide the missing interactive, relational, and contemplative layer**: wrapping around DP Education videos with **Gurudevi’s local-first teacher lesson planning tools, Socratic peer inquiry circles, and the Thuthimal gratitude network** to transform passive screen consumption into vibrant human community life.
