# Innovation Dossier: Calm Classroom (The Luster Learning 3-Minute Somatic Restoration Script)

> **Category**: 3. Contemplative Pedagogy & Slow Education  
> **Origin / Custodian**: USA / Luster Learning Institute 501(c)(3) (Founded by Jai & Cheryl Luster, Chicago, 2007)  
> **Official Portal**: [calmclassroom.com](https://calmclassroom.com)

> **Scale**: Over 500,000 students and 35,000 teachers in urban public districts  
> **License**: Non-Profit Educational Commons / Schoolwide Somatic Script Framework

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

**Calm Classroom**, created by the Luster Learning Institute in Chicago, is a standardized, trauma-informed **daily somatic relaxation and breathwork protocol** engineered specifically to integrate into high-poverty, high-stress urban public school districts without requiring technology, expensive equipment, or changes to academic curricula.

Urban school environments frequently operate under continuous sensory assault: sirens outside, loud hallways, crowded cafeterias, and high-stakes test stress that keep both children and teachers locked in chronic sympathetic arousal (elevated heart rate, shallow chest breathing, hyper-reactivity). Calm Classroom institutes a **sacred daily rhythm**:
- **Three-Minute Micro-Sessions (3x Daily)**: Conducted at three pivotal transition moments: morning homeroom arrival, return from lunch/recess, and before afternoon dismissal.
- **Scripted Teacher-Led Somatic Rituals**: 35 standardized, simple exercises (Belly Breathing, Arm Reach & Stretch, Peaceful Place visualization, Gentle Neck Rolls, Palm Rubbing & Eye Cupping) read aloud by the classroom teacher in a soft, steady voice.
- **Universal Schoolwide Reset**: When an entire school participates simultaneously, hallway aggression drops dramatically, disciplinary referrals plunge by over 40%, and classroom focus is instantaneously restored.

## 2. Pedagogical Architecture & Somatic Categories

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ CALM CLASSROOM SOMATIC PROTOCOL TOPOLOGY                               │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Breathing Category: Diaphragmatic Balloon Breathing (Vagus nerve)    │
│ • Stretching Category: Seated Spinal Twist, Shoulder Roll Tension Melt │
│ • Focusing Category: Candle Flame & Sound Tracking (Prefrontal cortex) │
│ • Relaxation Category: Progressive Muscle Clench & Release, Palm Warmth│
│ • Time Budget: Exactly 3 minutes (180 seconds) per session             │
│ • Equipment: Zero technology (Printed card deck or soft teacher voice) │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **The Diaphragmatic "Belly Balloon"**:
   - Students sit upright with feet flat on the floor, placing one hand on their abdomen. As they inhale slowly through the nose for 4 counts, their belly expands like a balloon; as they exhale through the mouth for 6 counts, the belly deflates. The extended exhalation directly triggers the parasympathetic calming reflex.

2. **Palm Rubbing and Ocular Cupping**:
   - Students rub their palms together vigorously for 15 seconds to generate friction heat, then gently cup their warm palms over their closed eyes, taking three deep breaths in total darkness to relieve digital eye strain and soothe visual cortex fatigue.

3. **Student Leadership Rotation**:
   - After several weeks, students take turns standing at the front of the room to read the 3-minute script and ring the chime, turning emotional regulation into a celebrated peer leadership skill.

## 4. Synthesis & Key Lessons for EdLove & Gurudevi (Sri Lanka)

### Strengths to Emulate:
1. **The Power of Ultra-Short Consistency**: Proves that 3 minutes of somatic breathing practiced consistently three times a day has far greater neurological impact than a 60-minute yoga class once a month.
2. **Zero Tech Dependency**: Operates entirely through the human voice, breath, and body posture, immune to power cuts or technological failure.

### Limitations:
1. **Teacher Fidelity**: Requires school leadership to protect the 3-minute practice from being cut when teachers feel pressured to rush through syllabus material.

### ✦ Direct Grounding for Sri Lankan Morning Homeroom (*Udasana Pasala*):
- In Sri Lanka, the school day starts early (7:30 AM), with children often traveling hours in crowded buses or walking miles in tropical heat.
- EdLove and Gurudevi embed **Calm Classroom’s 3-minute somatic breathwork scripts directly into the morning interface**: providing Sri Lankan teachers with Sinhala and Tamil audio scripts and prompts to settle the classroom into peaceful, focused presence every morning.
