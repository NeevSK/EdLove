## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

**LibreTexts Open Education Network** represents a significant milestone within **14. Open Educational Resources & Open Access (13 Entries)**.

### Core Tenet:
Multi-institutional collaborative open textbook platform continually updating STEM and humanities curricula.

Historically, traditional educational institutions and digital systems have struggled with centralized extraction, administrative fatigue, or network fragility. LibreTexts Open Education Network addresses this by reimagining how educational interactions are mediated and sustained.
