## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

**Minetest Open Educational Sandbox** represents a significant milestone within **12. Spatial, Simulation & Physical Computing (12 Entries)**.

Infinite voxel world game engine providing an open-source, moddable alternative to commercial Minecraft.
