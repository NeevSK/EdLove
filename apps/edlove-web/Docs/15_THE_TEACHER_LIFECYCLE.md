## 🧠 THE COGNITIVE SOVEREIGNTY: Owning the Mind
*Moving from consuming information to managing knowledge.*

### 1. The Student "Second Brain"
- **Obsidian / Logseq**: Teaching students to build local knowledge graphs (No cloud, total privacy).
- **Notion for Kids**: Creating a digital headquarters for every school project.
- **PKM (Personal Knowledge Management)**: The shift from "Memorizing for a test" to "Building a library for life."

### 2. Sovereign Identity (Self-Sovereign Education)
- **DID (Decentralized IDs)**: Students owning their own graduation certificates on the blockchain (Immutable & Portable).
- **Verifiable Credentials**: Proving you have a skill (e.g., Coding or Nursing) without needing a central university's stamp.

*"To build a passage, one must first own their own ground."*

- **The side-hustle is the standard, not the exception.**
