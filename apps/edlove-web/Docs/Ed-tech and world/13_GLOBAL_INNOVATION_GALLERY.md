## 🔍 Synthesis for The Passage

1.  **Beauty in Simplicity**: The most impactful tools in Africa/Asia are often low-bandwidth (WhatsApp, SMS).
2.  **Sovereignty in Tech**: Australia's Indigenous-owned tech shows us how to own the "Digital Reality".
3.  **The AI Shield**: Estonia and South Korea are showing us how to use AI as a *companion*, not a replacement.
