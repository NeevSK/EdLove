## PURPOSE

Education transformation is not a solo journey. No vision survives without the people who carry it together.

The Sangha is the human layer. The space where educators — and anyone who cares about how we learn — find each other. Not for networking. Not for performance. For **solidarity, wisdom-sharing, and collective becoming**.

When someone arrives here, they should feel: *"I'm not alone in this. There are others who see what I see. And we can figure this out together."*

---

## WHAT "SANGHA" MEANS

Sangha is a Sanskrit/Pali word meaning **community of practitioners**. Originally from Buddhism — the community of people walking a path together, supporting each other's growth.

We chose this word deliberately. The Sangha is not a forum. It is not a social network. It is a **community of people practicing** — practicing seeing education differently, practicing making change, practicing being human in a system that often dehumanizes.

---

## WHO IS THE SANGHA FOR?

- **Educators**: Teachers, facilitators, mentors — anyone who teaches in any form
- **Parents and families**: People making educational decisions for their children
- **Students**: Young people questioning their own education
- **Policymakers**: People with power to change systems
- **Curious people**: Anyone who cares about how we learn and grow
- **Gurudevi users**: Teachers using the app who want peer support

**The rule**: If you care about education — you belong here.

## THE DISCORD STRUCTURE (If Launching With Discord)

Discord is free and works well for community. If we launch with it, here's the structure:

### Channels

**Welcome & Start Here:**
- `#welcome` — What the Sangha is. How to be here. The principles.
- `#introductions` — Tell us who you are. What brought you here.

**By Context:**
- `#sri-lanka-educators` — For educators in Sri Lanka specifically
- `#paradigm-explorations` — Discussing the different ways of education (from Domain I)
- `#gurudevi-users` — For people using the Gurudevi app

**By Need:**
- `#questions` — Ask anything. Someone probably knows. Or we'll figure it out together.
- `#resources-requests` — Need something for your school? Post here. (Connects to Domain IV)
- `#stories` — Share your education story. The real one.

**By Interest:**
- `#policy-and-change` — For people interested in systemic change
- `#research-and-ideas` — For the intellectually curious
- `#parents-and-families` — For parents navigating education choices

**Meta:**
- `#feedback` — Tell us how to make this community better
- `#announcements` — Updates from edlove (read-only)

### Moderation Principles
- **No shame**: People are here because they're questioning something. That takes courage. Honor it.
- **No gatekeeping**: You don't need credentials to belong here. Lived experience counts.
- **No arguments for the sake of arguments**: Disagree? Do it with curiosity, not aggression.
- **No spam or self-promotion**: This is not a marketing channel.

## FUTURE EVOLUTION (Phase 2-3)

### Phase 2: Custom Forum
- **Discourse** (open source, self-hosted) replaces Discord
- Threaded conversations (better for deep discussion than Discord)
- Searchable history (Discord search is terrible)
- Topic categories that mirror edlove domains
- User profiles with their education journey

### Phase 3: Full Community Platform
- **Local hubs**: Geographic meetups, both virtual and in-person
- **Mentorship matching**: Connect experienced educators with those starting out
- **Project showcases**: Community members share what they're building
- **Events**: Workshops, webinars, conferences (free and paid)
- **Collaborative projects**: Groups working together on education initiatives
- **Youth forum**: A specific space for students (with appropriate moderation)

## THE SANGHA'S ROLE IN THE ECOSYSTEM

The Sangha is not just a nice-to-have. It is **structurally important**:

- **Domain II (Sri Lanka)** needs community stories to fill in the shadow education map
- **Domain IV (Resources)** needs community members to surface needs and offers
- **Domain VI (Epistemic Core)** needs community wisdom to complement academic research
- **Domain III (Gurudevi)** needs peer support for teachers using the app

**The Sangha feeds every other domain. It is the living root system beneath the visible ecosystem.**
