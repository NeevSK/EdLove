# Editorial Sanctuary Layout & High-Craft Reading Experience

## 1. Architectural Philosophy: The Contemplative Reading Sanctuary

An essay in EdLove or Gurudevi is not a standard web document; it is a sacred space for quiet, unhurried thought. The layout must reflect the craftsmanship of fine bookbinding, typography, and architectural stillness.

---

## 2. Core Pillars of Elevated Editorial Craft

### A. Minimalist Header & Progressive Provenance
* **Above-the-Fold Calm**: Replace heavy metadata tables at the top with a singular, refined byline (`Author · Date · Reading Time · Cadence Seal`).
* **The Colophon**: Deep provenance details (version, license, custody, DOI/canonical) are housed in a formal **Colophon** at the base of the essay.

### B. High-End Editorial Typography
* **Drop Cap Initial (`.prose-drop-cap`)**: A 3-line tall Fraunces / Lora initial serif on the opening paragraph to visually anchor the eye.
* **Ornamental Section Breaks**: Replacing generic `<hr>` rules with delicate glyph dividers (`✦ · ✦ · ✦`).
* **Golden Proportions**: Body text set to $1.125\text{rem}$ ($18\text{px}$) with $1.75$ line-height across a strict $68\text{ch}$ measure.

### C. Ambient Reading Progress
* **Golden Hairline (`#reading-progress`)**: A $1.5\text{px}$ golden thread at the top edge of the viewport tracking reading scroll position with zero performance overhead.

### D. Colophon & Reciprocal Journey Footer
* **Author Card**: Short biography, mission, and related essays.
* **Colophon Drawer**: Complete formal citation, open-access licensing, and translation registry.
