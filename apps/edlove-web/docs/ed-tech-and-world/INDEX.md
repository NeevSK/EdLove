## 🏛️ Epistemic Synthesis for EdLove & Gurudevi

Through this 100-entry global foraging, we find that human ingenuity has built powerful answers for isolated challenges:
1. **Offline & Low-Bandwidth**: Solutions exist in *Kolibri*, *Simba*, and *Beekee Box*.
2. **Pedagogical Slowness & Reverence**: Deeply articulated by *Slow Education UK*, *Tagore's Visva-Bharati*, and *Krishnamurti*.
3. **Teacher Autonomy & Anti-Surveillance**: Prototyped by *iDoceo* and *ScheduleMaker*.
4. **Gift Economics & Reciprocity**: Explored by *Ecobasa* and *LearnReciprocal*.

**EdLove & Gurudevi's sovereign mandate** is bringing these four dimensions together into a single, cohesive, local-first ecosystem for Sri Lanka and the Global South.
