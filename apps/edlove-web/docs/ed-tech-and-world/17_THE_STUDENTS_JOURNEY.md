## 💎 THE STUDENT'S FIRST PRINCIPLES
1.  **You are the Architect**: You don't just "receive" education; you design it.
2.  **The Second Brain is Real**: You shouldn't have to keep everything in your head. Build your own library.
3.  **To Teach is to Learn**: The best way to master a skill is to help a peer.
4.  **Ownership matters**: Your grades, your certificates, and your data belong to *you*, not the school.
5.  **Focus is a Skill**: Your attention is your most valuable asset. Protect it.

*"The student is not a vessel to be filled, but a fire to be kindled."*
*(and the technology should be the fuel, not the cage.)*
