## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

**Otter.ai Accessible Transcripts** represents a significant milestone within **11. Accessible, Inclusive & Neurodiverse Tech (12 Entries)**.

### Core Tenet:
Real-time speech-to-text live transcription supporting deaf and hard-of-hearing students in lecture halls.

Historically, traditional educational institutions and digital systems have struggled with centralized extraction, administrative fatigue, or network fragility. Otter.ai Accessible Transcripts addresses this by reimagining how educational interactions are mediated and sustained.
