# THE PASSAGE: Sri Lanka Friendly EdTech

This document curates tools that are **globally accessible**, mostly **free or freemium**, and optimized for the **Sri Lankan context** (low bandwidth, mobile-first, and syllabus-aligned).

## 🚀 Global Essentials for the SL Teacher
*The most accessible global tools that simplify the 'Educator Lifecycle' in Sri Lanka.*

### For the Classroom (Process)
- **WhatsApp**: The king of comms. Use for distribution of PDFs and voice-memo lessons.
- **ZipGrade**: **Game Changer.** Grade 100 MCQ papers in the classroom with no internet. [ZipGrade](https://zipgrade.com)
- **Canva Education**: Free premium designs for school festivals and certificates. [Canva](https://canva.com/education)
- **MagicSchool AI**: Rapidly create lesson plans for the National Curriculum. [MagicSchool](https://magicschool.ai)

### For the Teacher (Life)
- **Calm Schools**: Free meditation to manage the stress of large classes. [Calm](https://calm.com/schools)
- **UNESCO GTC**: Professional development that results in international certificates. [GTC](https://unesco.org/gtc)
- **Google Gemini**: A creative partner for Sinhala/Tamil/English lesson content. [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com)

## 📶 Low-Bandwidth & Offline Strategies
*How to keep learning alive when the internet is intermittent.*

| Strategy / Tool | Solution | Logic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **KA-Lite** | **Offline Khan Academy** | Install on a server/laptop for local Wi-Fi use without data. |
| **ZipGrade** | **Offline MCQ Grading** | No internet needed to grade papers in the classroom. |
| **YouTube Offline**| Curated Video Playlists | Download at school, watch at home. |
| **SD Cards** | Resource Delivery | Pre-loading "Digital Boxes" with PDFs and videos. |
