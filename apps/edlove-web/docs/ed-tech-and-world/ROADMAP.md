### Non-Negotiable Requirements per Dossier:
1. **Real Verified URLs & Origins**: Exact official websites, GitHub repos, or foundation links (NO generic Google search placeholders).
2. **Deep Technical & Architectural Specifics**:
   - Exact languages, databases, protocols (e.g., Python/Django, SQLite, CRDTs, LoRa, Drupal module schemas, Canvas/WebGL engines, static HTML dithering).
   - Concrete network topology (offline mesh, air-gapped USB sneakernet, local Wi-Fi hotspots, zero-data SMS).
3. **True Economic Mechanics**:
   - Exact funding models (pure gift economy, quadratic public goods funding, one-time fair licensing, grant commons, time-banking).
4. **Contextual Grounding for EdLove & Sri Lanka**:
   - Specific analysis of how the innovation applies to Sri Lankan ground realities (provincial vs national school disparities, Rs. 210Bn shadow tuition machine, low-bandwidth rural conditions, *Thuthimal* gift loops).
5. **Docs-SOP Compliance**:
