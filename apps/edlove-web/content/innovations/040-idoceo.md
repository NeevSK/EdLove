# Innovation Dossier: iDoceo (The Sovereign Offline Teacher Gradebook)

> **Category**: 4. Teacher Sovereignty & Calm Instruments  
> **Origin**: Spain / Independent Software Developer  
> **Platform**: iPad, iPhone, macOS  
> **Official Portal**: [idoceo.net](https://www.idoceo.net)  
> **Business Model**: One-time purchase / Fair Indie Licensing

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

**iDoceo** is universally recognized among independent educators as the premier benchmark for **private, local-storage gradebook, planner, and classroom management software**.

Unlike Google Classroom or district-mandated ERP systems that harvest student analytics or mandate internet connectivity, iDoceo was built from day one on the principle that **a teacher’s gradebook belongs to the teacher, on their own device, in absolute privacy**.

---

## 2. Key Capabilities & Technical Strengths

1. **100% Local SQLite Database**: All student records, grades, seating charts, rubrics, and diary reflections are stored directly in the local file system.
2. **Zero Mandatory Cloud Connectivity**: Works flawlessly in classrooms with zero Wi-Fi or behind strict firewall blocks.
3. **Deep Visual Customization**: Interactive 2D seating plans with student photos, custom calculation engines (weighted formulas, GPA, conditional color formatting), and audio/video note attachments.
4. **Non-Extractive Business Model**: Built without subscription traps, ad banners, or data selling.

---

## 3. Critical Assessment & Direct Lessons for Gurudevi

### Strengths:
- Demonstrates massive global teacher loyalty when software respects teacher autonomy and eliminates administrative surveillance.

### Limitations:
- **Apple-Only Silo**: Limited exclusively to iOS/macOS, making it inaccessible to the vast majority of educators in the Global South (like Sri Lanka, where Android represents >85% of mobile devices).
- **Single-User Isolation**: Does not connect the teacher with peer educators or community support loops.

### ✦ Direct Takeaway for Gurudevi:
Gurudevi takes the **sovereign, local-database ethos of iDoceo** and makes it **democratically accessible on low-cost Android phones** via React Native, MMKV, and PowerSync, while adding the **Thuthimal community gift-loop**.
