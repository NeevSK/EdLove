# Innovation Dossier: Nenasa TV & Smart School Initiative (Dialog Axiata & Sri Lanka MOE Satellite/Mobile Bridge)

> **Category**: 6. National & Provincial Digital Leaps  
> **Origin / Custodian**: Sri Lanka / Dialog Axiata PLC & Ministry of Education (MOE Sri Lanka, 2009)  
> **Official Initiative Portal**: [dialog.lk/nenasa-tv](https://www.dialog.lk/nenasa-tv) / [nenasa.lk](https://nenasa.lk)  
> **Infrastructure Deployment**: Direct-to-Home (DTH) Satellite Television (Ku-Band), Nenasa Mobile App, Nenasa Smart Schools Network  
> **Physical Footprint**: 2,000+ Rural Government Schools equipped with free satellite decoders, high-gain dishes, and 32" TV units  
> **Scale**: Over 1.5 million Sri Lankan students reached across all 9 provinces  
> **License**: Public-Private Partnership (CSR / Universal Service Obligation Framework)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

**Nenasa** (meaning *"Eye of Wisdom"* in Sinhala), founded in 2009 as a landmark Public-Private Partnership between telecommunications giant **Dialog Axiata** and the **Sri Lankan Ministry of Education (MOE)**, is Sri Lanka's pioneering **national satellite television and digital smart school infrastructure network**.

Following the end of the civil conflict in 2009, rural schools in the Northern, Eastern, and remote Central provinces suffered from extreme infrastructure destruction, power grid volatility, and severe shortages of specialized G.C.E. Ordinary Level (O/L) and Advanced Level (A/L) subject teachers. Nenasa was architected to bypass terrestrial terrestrial cable and internet infrastructure through **direct satellite broadcasting**:
- **8 Dedicated DTH Satellite Educational Channels**: Broadcast 24/7 in Sinhala, Tamil, and English, directly aligned with the National Institute of Education (NIE) syllabus for Grades 1 through 13.
- **2,000 Free Rural School Satellite Installations**: Dialog installed and maintains Ku-band satellite dishes, solar-compatible decoders, and commercial television monitors inside rural government schools at zero cost to the state.
- **Nenasa Smart Schools Transformation**: Transformed 100+ public schools into digital hubs with interactive whiteboards, teacher ICT training, and the **Nenasa Education Mobile App**.

```
│ • Teacher Network: 3,000+ Trained ICT Ambassadors across 9 Provinces   │
│ • Interactive Subsystem: 1377 Nenasa Interactive Voice Response (IVR)  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Direct-to-Home (DTH) Satellite Broadcast Resilience**:
   - Ku-Band satellite broadcasting is immune to terrestrial fiber cuts, monsoon landslides, and remote mountain geography—delivering crystal-clear 1080i video signals to schools situated deep in tea estates or jungle villages with zero internet connectivity.

2. **The 1377 Tele-Education IVR Hotline**:
   - Implemented an Interactive Voice Response (IVR) phone service enabling students with basic feature phones (2G) to dial toll-free to listen to audio lessons, practice past paper multiple-choice questions, and hear spoken English pronunciation drills.

3. **Nenasa Smart Schools ICT Teacher Training**:
   - Partnered with Microsoft and the Ministry of Education to run multi-week digital pedagogy certifications, training over 3,000 public school teachers to integrate multimedia presentations and interactive quizzes into daily classroom routines.

## 4. Synthesis & Key Lessons for EdLove & Gurudevi (Sri Lanka)

### Strengths to Emulate:
1. **The Sovereignty of Satellite Direct Broadcast**: Proves that satellite TV remains the ultimate robust broadcast medium for reaching 100% of geographically isolated schools when internet grids fail.
2. **Enduring Public-Private Partnership**: Demonstrating how corporate telecommunication infrastructure can be harnessed for enduring public educational benefit over 15+ continuous years.

### Limitations:
1. **Passive Transmission Mode**: Satellite TV is fundamentally a linear, one-directional broadcast; students cannot ask live questions or receive immediate interactive feedback.

### ✦ Direct Synergy with EdLove & Gurudevi:
- Nenasa provides the **resilient national physical satellite receiver network and studio broadcast foundation** across 2,000+ rural Sri Lankan schools.
- **Gurudevi and EdLove provide the complementary local-first software layer**: running on teachers' mobile devices in those exact same 2,000 schools, turning satellite TV viewing sessions into **vibrant Socratic dialogues, peer problem swarms, and Thuthimal community gratitude circles**.
