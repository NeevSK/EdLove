## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

The **Kalu Ganga Bio-Regional Field Labs** is EdLove’s flagship Sri Lankan **place-based, watershed-scale ecological and computational learning architecture**, designed to ground secondary science, mathematics, and geography education directly in the living, breathing reality of **Sri Lanka's most sacred and torrential river basin: the Kalu Ganga**.

Traditional Sri Lankan schooling teaches river hydrology, climate science, and biology out of abstract, monochrome government textbooks—forcing children who live 100 meters from a riverbank to memorize foreign diagrams while remaining blind to their own local environmental threats (monsoon flash floods, illegal gem mining siltation, riverbank erosion, microplastic pollution). The Kalu Ganga Field Labs invert this:
- **The River as the Core Curriculum**: The entire 129km river system—from the pristine cloud rainforest headwaters at Sri Pada through the gem-rich valleys of Ratnapura down to the ocean estuary at Kalutara—serves as an open, living scientific laboratory.
- **Ancient *Ellangawa* (Cascade Tank) Hydrological Science**: Integrating 2,000-year-old Sinhala hydraulic engineering principles (*Wewa, Bisokotuwa, Ralapanawa*) with modern micro-controller water sensors and GIS mapping.
- **Youth as Sovereign Environmental Guardians**: Rural students take real scientific responsibility: monitoring river water turbidity, dissolved oxygen, and macroinvertebrate biodiversity, publishing verified open data to protect their communities from floods and pollution.

## 2. Pedagogical Architecture & The Watershed Field Stations

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ KALU GANGA BIO-REGIONAL FIELD LAB ARCHITECTURE                         │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Headwaters Station (Sri Pada Foothills): Cloud forest micro-climates │
│ • Mid-Basin Station (Ratnapura Valleys): Gem geology & flood hydrology │
│ • Lower-Basin Station (Millaniya/Horana): Agricultural runoff & wetlands│
│ • Estuary Station (Kalutara): Brackish mangrove ecosystems & salinity  │
│ • Hardware Sensor Mesh: Solar micro:bit water turbidity & temp sensors │
│ • Data Pipeline: Local-first SQLite/Nitro sync via Gurudevi Mobile     │
│ • Cultural Memory: Oral histories of river boatmen (*Pāruwa* culture) │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **The 4-Station Longitudinal Inquiry**:
   - Schools along the Kalu Ganga coordinate longitudinal science projects: students in Kalutara compare the water salinity and microbial health of their estuary with data transmitted by high school students in the Ratnapura hill country upstream.

2. **Low-Cost Hardware Sensor Kits**:
   - Students build their own submersible water testing probes using BBC micro:bit boards, Arduino sensors, and recycled plastic casings: measuring electrical conductivity, pH, water temperature, and dissolved oxygen.

3. **Reviving the Lost *Pāruwa* (River Barge) Cultural Memory**:
   - Combines hard hydrology with indigenous anthropology: students interview surviving village elders who navigated traditional wooden river freight barges (*Kalu Ganga Pāruwa*), preserving river folklore, songlines, and traditional fishing customs.

## 4. Synthesis & Key Lessons for EdLove & Gurudevi (Sri Lanka)

### Strengths & Core Breakthroughs:
1. **Rooted Bio-Regional Consciousness**: Instilling a deep, lifelong emotional reverence for the local land and water that sustains the children’s families.
2. **Integrating High Tech with Indigenous Wisdom**: Seamlessly uniting modern IoT sensor microcontrollers with ancient Sinhala hydraulic heritage.

### ✦ The Macro Synthesis for EdLove:
The Kalu Ganga Bio-Regional Field Labs represent the physical and ecological heart of the EdLove pedagogical mission in Sri Lanka. By transforming Sri Lanka’s rivers, rainforests, and tank cascades into living classrooms, EdLove restores the ancient covenant between human intelligence, community care, and the living earth.
