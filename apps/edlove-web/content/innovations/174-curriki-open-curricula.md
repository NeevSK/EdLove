## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

**Curriki Open Learning Curricula** represents a significant milestone within **14. Open Educational Resources & Open Access (13 Entries)**.

### Core Tenet:
Global online community for teachers to share, adapt, and build interactive open digital courses.

Historically, traditional educational institutions and digital systems have struggled with centralized extraction, administrative fatigue, or network fragility. Curriki Open Learning Curricula addresses this by reimagining how educational interactions are mediated and sustained.
