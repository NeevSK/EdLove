## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

**Wildflower Montessori Network** represents a significant milestone within **13. Micro-Schools, Forest Schools & Democratic Models (13 Entries)**.

Decentralized ecosystem of shop-front micro-schools operated collaboratively by teacher-leaders.
