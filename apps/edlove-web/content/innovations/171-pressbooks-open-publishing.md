## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

**Pressbooks Open Textbook Publisher** represents a significant milestone within **14. Open Educational Resources & Open Access (13 Entries)**.

### Core Tenet:
Open-source WordPress-based publishing platform enabling faculty and teachers to author multi-format textbooks.

Historically, traditional educational institutions and digital systems have struggled with centralized extraction, administrative fatigue, or network fragility. Pressbooks Open Textbook Publisher addresses this by reimagining how educational interactions are mediated and sustained.
