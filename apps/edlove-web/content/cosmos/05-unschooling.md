### Beyond the Classroom

Unschooling removes the boundary between "life" and "school." Learning is not something that happens at a desk; it's something that happens because you are alive and curious.

#### Core Logic
- **Natural Learning**: Following a child's obsession—whether it's dinosaurs, coding, or baking—wherever it leads.
- **Trust**: Deep respect for the learner's intrinsic drive.
- **Community as Resource**: The whole world becomes the learning environment.

#### The Privilege Tension
Requires highly engaged caregivers and often relies on resources not accessible to everyone.
