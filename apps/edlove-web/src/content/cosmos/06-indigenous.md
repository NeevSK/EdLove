### Relational Wisdom

Indigenous education predates civilization by millions of years. It is holistic, relational, and deeply tied to place.

#### Core Principles
- **Land as Teacher**: Knowledge is not abstract; it's rooted in the living world.
- **Elders as Curriculum**: Intergenerational transmission of stories, skills, and values.
- **Holistic**: No separation between intellect, spirit, and survival.

#### The Struggle
Systematically dismantled by colonization, this ancient wisdom is now being reclaimed as essential for a sustainable future.
