### The Original Paradigm

Education predates civilization by millions of years. It is the wisdom of life teaching life.

#### Beyond Humanity
- **Birds**: Teaching their young to fly.
- **Elephants**: Passing down navigation and social behaviors across generations.
- **Bees**: Communicating location through complex dance.

#### The Living Stream
Learning is an organic unfolding. We are part of this living stream, not separate from it. True education honors this biological root.
