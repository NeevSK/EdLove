We are part of the living world, not separate from it. For 99% of human history, "education" did not exist as a separate institution. Learning was simply *living*—an organic unfolding of wisdom in harmony with the environment.

### The Ocean vs. The Pool
You do not learn the depth of the ocean by swimming in a heated, chlorinated pool. While the pool (the classroom) offers safety, it lacks the currents, the tides, and the salt of reality. We learn to swim in life by being *in* life—watching the elders, feeling the buoyancy, and navigating the waves ourselves. True wisdom is found in the immersion, not the simulation.

### Core Principles
- **Contextual**: Learning happens in the deep water of existence.
- **Social**: We learn to stay afloat by watching and mirroring the school.
- **Embodied**: Buoyancy is felt in the bones before it is understood by the brain.

### Advanced Thought: "This Is Water"
As David Foster Wallace reminded us, the most obvious and important realities are often the hardest to see and talk about. Like fish in the ocean who don't know what "water" is, we are often blind to the medium of *Life* that sustains our learning. Modern schooling tries to bottle the water; we aim to return the learner to the sea.
