### The Paradigm Most People Grew Up In

The Industrial Model was never designed for human flourishing. It was designed for industrial output.

#### Origins
Born during the Industrial Revolution to produce workers for factories. It emphasized punctuality, obedience, and repetitive tasks.

#### Core Features
- **Age-Based Sorting**: Children as "batches" in a factory line.
- **Bell-Schedule**: Synchronized movements across the entire institution.
- **Standardized Testing**: Quality control for human output.

#### Tensions
While it provided scale and structure, it often crushes curiosity and defines intelligence so narrowly that many beautiful minds are left behind.
