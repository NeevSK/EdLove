Born to serve the needs of industrialization, the traditional school model was designed to produce standardized workers. The goal was simple: individuals who could follow complex instructions, tolerate boredom, and respect hierarchy.

### Transitioning Out
This model is not "broken"—it is just obsolete. It achieved exactly what it was designed for. However, in an age of AI and complex global challenges, compliance is no longer the primary virtue. We need sovereignty, not standardization.
