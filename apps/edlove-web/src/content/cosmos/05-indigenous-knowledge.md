Knowledge is not an object to be possessed; it is a relationship to be maintained. For indigenous cultures, education is inseparable from the land, the ancestors, and the responsibility to future generations.

### Integration
This paradigm rejects the Western separation of spirit and matter, or mind and body. It emphasizes the integration of all aspects of being and the importance of lineage. We learn from the earth as much as we learn from each other.
