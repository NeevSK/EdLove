Education is not a transaction; it is a resonance. "Teaning" (Teacher + Learning) captures the impedance-matched circuit where teaching and learning dissolve into a single flow of becoming.

### The Resonance Circuit
When the student's curiosity matches the teacher's presence, the administrative noise of the classroom disappears. This framework encourages educators to focus on the *quality of connection* rather than the *quantity of content*.
