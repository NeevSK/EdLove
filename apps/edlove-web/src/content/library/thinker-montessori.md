A medical doctor who became one of history's most influential educationalists. Her work in the "Casa dei Bambini" proved that when provided with a scientifically prepared environment, children display profound concentration and self-discipline.

### Key Contribution
The discovery of the **Absorbent Mind**—the unique mental power of the young child to absorb knowledge directly from their environment without conscious effort.
