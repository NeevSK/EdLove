While our ultimate goal is an algorithmic ecosystem, we begin with a human-led, manual matchmaking process. This ensures that every transfer of value is grounded in trust and genuine relationship.

### The Three-Step Pulse
1. **Identify Need**: Teachers surface what they truly need to protect their focus.
2. **Pledge Resource**: Allies offer what they are willing to share.
3. **Facilitate Transfer**: We bridge the gap with zero overhead.
