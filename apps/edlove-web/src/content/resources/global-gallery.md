### A Global Scan of Innovation

The Global Gallery is a curated network of educational beauty from every corner of the world. It maps country-specific transformations and unique local solutions.

#### 🌍 Africa: The Mobile Renaissance
*Focus: Accessibility and Resourcefulness.*
Tools like **Foondamate** (WhatsApp bots in SA) and **Eneza Education** (SMS learning in Kenya) show how high impact can be achieved with low-bandwidth tech.

#### 🌏 Asia: The AI Frontier
*Focus: Scale and Personalization.*
From South Korea's **AI Textbooks** (launching 2025) to India's **Vernacular AI** mentors, the continent is leading the integration of high-tech with local languages.

#### 🇪🇺 Europe: The Human-Centric Leap
*Focus: Ethics and Teacher Empowerment.*
Estonia's **AI Leap** provides AI chatbots to every high schooler as a fundamental digital right, emphasizing digital maturity.

#### 🌎 The Americas: Immersive & Social
*Focus: VR/AR and Gamification.*
Uruguay's **Ceibal** platform and Brazil's **Yuna Stories** use immersive tech to ensure the child is the hero of their own learning journey.
