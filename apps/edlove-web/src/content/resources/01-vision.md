Resource flow determines reality. In the current educational landscape, resources are often trapped in bureaucratic filters, with high "skimming" and low direct impact. We propose a direct inversion: a peer-to-peer network where demand signals from teachers are met directly by supply.

### Direct Connection
A sanctuary where zero skimming is the standard. Whether it is pedagogical expertise, material goods, or philanthropic capital, the resource should flow to where the "curiosity-need" is most acute.
