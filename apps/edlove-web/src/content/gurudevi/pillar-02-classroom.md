The classroom is your domain, but administrative chaos often invades it. This pillar constructs a digital perimeter around your teaching, automating the trivial so you can focus on the profound.

### Reclaiming the Zone
From attendance to grading, the Pillar II tools are designed to be "invisible infrastructure." They handle the logistics so that you can return to the state of "Teaning"—where learning flows without friction.
