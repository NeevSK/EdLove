Before you are a teacher—before the roles, the grading, the expectations—you are a human being with a nervous system that needs tending. The "Self" pillar is not about productivity; it is about preservation.

### Radical Preservation
In a system that extracts labor, your act of resistance is to remain whole. This pillar provides somatic grounding, micro-meditations, and the "Dignity Log" to help you maintain your internal sanctuary amidst external chaos.
