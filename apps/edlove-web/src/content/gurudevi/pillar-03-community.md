The Sangha is where the individual's practice finds its collective strength. This pillar connects your sanctuary with others, creating a peer-to-peer network of resonance rather than a hierarchy of extraction.

### Relational Clarity
We cultivate a space where teachers support teachers. From shared wisdom to collective advocacy, Pillar III facilitates the emergence of a self-governing community rooted in trust and mutual dignity.
