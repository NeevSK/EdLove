The teacher is the heart of the educational ecosystem. In Sri Lanka, the "Guru" has historically been a figure of immense respect. However, modern administrative systems have often reduced teachers to bureaucratic cogs.

### The Path Forward
Gurudevi and EdLove aim to restore the dignity and sovereignty of the teacher. By providing tools and communities that protect the teacher's focus, we allow the sacred act of teaching to flourish once again in our schools.
