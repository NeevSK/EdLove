### The Gap Between Promise and Reality

The tension between the Government system and the Shadow system creates a unique set of challenges for Sri Lankan education.

#### Key Discrepancies
- **Resource Flow**: While government funding is fixed, private money pours into tuition, widening the inequality gap.
- **Curriculum vsents**: The formal curriculum is often bypassed in favor of "exam-shortcuts" taught in tuition.
- **Teacher Dignity**: Teachers are often caught between their institutional duties and the lucrative tuition market.

#### The Impact
Students spend 14-16 hours a day in "education," yet the gap between their certificates and their real-world capabilities remains wide.
