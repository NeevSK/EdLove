### The Parallel Reality

While the government system provides the schools, the "Shadow" system—Tuition—is where much of the actual competition happens. It is a massive, unregulated market.

#### The Tuition Economy
- **Mass Classes**: Hundreds or thousands of students in a single hall, led by "Superstar" teachers.
- **Small Groups**: Highly localized, often in teacher's homes or private centers.
- **Posters Everywhere**: The visual landscape of Sri Lankan towns is dominated by tuition advertisements.

#### The Pressure
Tuition is no longer just "remedial"; it has become a prerequisite for success. It places immense financial and psychological pressure on families.
