### The Official Architecture

Sri Lanka's education system is one of the oldest in Asia, built on the promise of free education for all. It is a highly centralized, hierarchical structure.

#### The Hierarchy
- **National Level**: The Ministry of Education (Isurupaya) sets the policy and curriculum.
- **Provincial/Zonal Level**: Implementation and administration of thousands of schools.
- **The Schools**: Over 10,000 government schools serving millions of students.

#### The Promised Outcome
The formal system aims for uniform delivery and national standards, culminating in the highly competitive O/L and A/L examinations.
