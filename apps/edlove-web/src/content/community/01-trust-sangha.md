Teaching is often a solitary act performed in a crowded room. The Sangha breaks this isolation. It is a horizontal network of trust, free from the vertical judgment of administrative hierarchy.

### The Power of Presence
In the Sangha, we don't just share "best practices"—we share our presence. We recognize that the wellbeing of the teacher is the wellbeing of the student. By healing together, we transform the educational field itself.
