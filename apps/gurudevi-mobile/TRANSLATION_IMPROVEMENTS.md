## 🎨 Translation Philosophy

### Current Tone: **Formal, Literal**
- Sounds like textbook
- Too poetic/heavy
- Not conversational

### Target Tone: **Warm, Conversational**
- Like a friend talking
- Simple, clear words
- Inviting, not imposing

---

## 📝 Key Principles for Natural Translations

### 1. **Use Common Words**
❌ "සත්‍යය" (satya - truth - formal)  
✅ "හිත" (hita - heart/feeling - common)

### 2. **Active > Passive**
❌ "විදුවියන්නට" (to be released - passive)  
✅ "ඉවත් කරන්න" (remove - active)

### 3. **Short > Long**
❌ "ඔබගේ අවබෝධයේ උද්‍යානය" (garden of your understanding - 6 words)  
✅ "ඔබේ අදහස්" (your thoughts - 2 words)

### 4. **Warm > Cold**
❌ "ප්‍රජාව" (community - formal)  
✅ "පවුල" (family - warm)
