### **Why This Matters for Business Model**

**Most edtech treats both regimes identically** → fails in both.

**Thermodynamically sound approach**:
1. ✅ **Serve maintenance mode** (70%) with energy-returning features (FREE)
2. ✅ **Monetize optimization mode** (30%) with efficiency features (PAID)
3. ✅ **Redirect surplus** via philanthropy (OBAs, grants, CSR)

**This is not "generous pricing"—it's the only model that respects energy constraints.**

## 1.3 The Core Insight

> **"Education is not a neutral good. It is a high-energy symbolic amplifier. Used in the wrong thermodynamic context, it sharpens gradients."**

**What this means for Gurudevi**:

❌ **We do NOT promise**:
- Improve student outcomes (adds pressure in surplus-constrained system)
- Increase teacher productivity (more work, not more energy)
- Digitize workflows (change burden without buffering)
- Compete in credential race (intensifies gradient)

✅ **We DO promise**:
- Return energy to exhausted teachers (thermodynamic restoration)
- Create sanctuary in maintenance mode (emotional buffering)
- Connect isolated teachers (distributed support network)
- Recognize invisible labor (dignity in struggle)

**We buffer the humans crushed by the system, not optimize the system crushing humans.**
