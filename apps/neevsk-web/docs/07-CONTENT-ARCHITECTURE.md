### Mobile (< 640px)
- Hamburger menu
- Sections stack vertically
- Simplified interactions
- Touch-optimized

- Condensed nav
- 2-column grids where appropriate
- Full functionality

### Desktop (> 1024px)
- Full navigation
- Multi-column layouts
- Rich interactions
- Optimal reading width maintained

## 📝 Content Guidelines

### Voice & Tone
- **Personal:** Warm, vulnerable, reverent
- **Intellectual:** Wise, contemplative, clear
- **Professional:** Confident, clear, forward-thinking
- **Library:** Generous, curated, personal
- **Connect:** Optimistic, inviting, energized

### Language Principles
1. Speak directly, second person okay
2. Avoid jargon unless necessary
3. Be honest, not performative
4. Show depth without showing off
5. Warmth always present

### Content Length
- **Headlines:** Short, clear
- **Intros:** 2-3 sentences
- **Body:** As long as needed, no fluff
- **Descriptions:** Concise but complete
