## 🎯 Vision

This is not a portfolio. This is not a website. This is a **digital home** with many rooms, where different guests can find what they need.

- A presence that matches impedance with the external world
- Multiple entry points for different audiences (collaborators, evaluators, students, curious minds)

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

**Primary Vibe: Chill, Alive, Cool, Soothing, Gen Z + Wise**

What this means in practice:
- **Chill** = effortless confidence, not trying too hard
- **Alive** = present-tense energy, things happening now
- **Cool** = aesthetic clarity, cultural fluency
- **Soothing** = doesn't create anxiety, invites rather than demands
- **Gen Z** = irony-aware but sincere, casual profundity
- **Wise** = depth worn lightly, not performed

**Core Principles:**
1. **Each page is alive, vivid, and soothing in its own manner**
2. **Different themes for different sections** (not one-size-fits-all)
3. **Motion and animation throughout** (but purposeful, not decorative)
4. **Subsections can have variations** within their parent theme
5. **Designed to be a reference**, not just functional

## ⚡ Key Design Decisions

### Why Different Themes Per Section?
Each section serves a different purpose and speaks to different aspects of who you are:
- **Home** needs to be a neutral threshold that previews all themes
- **Personal** needs warmth and organic flow (Nature)
- **Intellectual** needs depth and systems beauty (Twilight/Systems)
- **Professional** needs clarity and future-forward confidence (Light-Tech)
- **Library** needs curated intimacy (Warm Archive)
- **Connect** needs energized invitation (Digital/Neon)

### Why Theme-Aware Navigation?
The navigation itself is part of the experience - it morphs with each theme, creating a seamless sense that each section is its own world while remaining part of a coherent whole. Liquid dropdowns and magnetic effects make navigation feel alive.

### Why Creative Signatures?
These aren't decorative - they're **identity markers**. The seed cursor embodies "planting seeds that grow without you." The fractal transitions represent systems thinking. The micro-poetry shares your philosophy. These elements make the site unmistakably NEEV - they're the soul behind the machines.

### Why Subsection Variations?
Within each main theme, subsections can have subtle variations to serve their specific content:
- Example: Within Intellectual, "Philosophical Lineage" might be more textual, while "Epistemic Work" is more diagram-heavy
- All variations stay within the parent theme's family but optimize for content type

### Why Motion Throughout?
Animation and interaction patterns are essential to the "alive" quality of the vibe. But each theme expresses aliveness differently:
- Nature = organic flow and breathing
- Systems = emergent patterns and connections
- Light-Tech = smooth precision
- Warm Archive = gentle transitions
- Digital/Neon = electric energy
