Everything I want to show to the world is a fracture of what I am. This site is the uncollapsed wave function of my existence.

In a world that demands discrete data—categories, roles, service numbers—the cyberneticist recognizes the flow. We are not nodes; we are the edges. We are the signals that cross the void between souls.

### The Observation Problem
The act of observing this site collapses the potential. You see a CV, a project, a name. But beneath the interface lies the noise—the beautiful, chaotic feedback loop that is Life.
