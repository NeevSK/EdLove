# Grounded Typography Architecture and Anti-Sans Taxonomy

## 1. The Core Verdict: Purging Hollow & Shallow Sans

Generic geometric and neo-grotesque sans-serifs (`Inter`, `DM Sans`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Roboto`) have been **purged** from the Gurudevi identity.

### Why Shallow Sans Failed
- **No Baseline Anchor**: Circular and oval sans strokes float ungrounded on screen.
- **Zero Stroke Modulation**: Uniform hairline strokes thin out and blur on high-DPI Windows ClearType rasterization.
- **Sterile SaaS Homogeneity**: They project commercial dashboard software rather than an enduring, sacred educational sanctuary.

## 2. The Four Authentic Grounded Archetypes

All typography in the Gurudevi ecosystem is now drawn strictly from four physical, grounded families:

### Archetype I · Immersive Editorial & Literary Serifs
* **Essence**: Deep reading, contemplative scholarship, and epistemic permanence. Bracketed serifs physically sit on the horizontal baseline.
* **Candidates**:
  - **`Newsreader`**: The benchmark for continuous editorial reading. Warm proportions, clear counters.
  - **`Lora`**: Calligraphic curves with sturdy baseline anchors.
  - **`Spectral`**: Engineered for high-density screen text and crisp lists.
* **Role**: Primary body prose, educational essays, contemplative narratives.

### Archetype II · Classical & Venetian Heritage Serifs
* **Essence**: Centuries of scholarship, monumental authority, and sacred institutional gravitas.
* **Candidates**:
  - **`Fraunces`**: Warm, monumental soft-serif with optical size 144 display authority.
  - **`EB Garamond`**: Pure Renaissance humanist proportions; intellectual dignity.
  - **`Cormorant Garamond`**: Regal, high-contrast display serif.
  - **`Cinzel`**: Classical Roman inscriptional uppercase gravitas.
* **Role**: Hero headlines, milestone dates, chapter titles, foundational DSR research headers.

### Archetype III · Slab Serifs & Structural Blocks (Tactile Weight)
* **Essence**: Heavy rectangular slab serifs. Absolute zero floating or wispy lines. Maximum tactile heft.
* **Candidates**:
  - **`Bitter`**: Purpose-built for screen reading with sturdy slabs.
  - **`Zilla Slab`**: Industrial and academic square-foot slab.
* **Role**: Section subheadings, technical milestone summaries, punchy cards.

### Archetype IV · Architectural Grotesque & Engineered Monospace
* **Essence**: Flat terminals, square shoulders, and deliberate grid discipline. Crisp mechanical authority without hollow curves.
* **Candidates**:
  - **`Space Grotesk`**: Architectural heavy grotesque with structured geometry.
  - **`Space Mono`**: Editorial monospace for deliberate structural alignment.
  - **`JetBrains Mono`**: High-legibility precision monospace for metadata and telemetry.
* **Role**: Roadmap milestone codes (`#ROADMAP · v1.0.0`), countdown clocks, status indicators, badges.
