# Gurudevi Copy and Content Guide

## Positioning

Gurudevi is an **education ecology support system** and a **sanctuary for education in Sri Lanka**. It is not only an LMS and not only a teacher wellbeing app. It supports the relationships between people, roles, workspaces, resources, and communities that make education possible.

Teachers remain the first-class design beneficiary because they carry a disproportionate operational and emotional load. The system is still polycentric: students, school leaders, administrators, and communities each have legitimate centers of work and agency.

## Core lines to develop and test

- A sanctuary for education in Sri Lanka.
- An education ecology support system.
- Technology that returns energy to the people who make education possible.
- One ecology, many centers of practice.
- Learning does not happen in isolation; it happens through relationships.

These are working lines, not claims of adoption or impact.

## Role language

| Role | Public explanation | Product surface |
|---|---|---|
| Teacher | The primary person we design around: teaching, care, preparation, and reflection without unnecessary extraction. | Classroom workspace |
| Student | A person with agency, curiosity, work, and a right to dignity—not a data point. | Learning Studio |
| Principal/admin | A school steward who needs coordination and visibility without turning care into surveillance. | School workspace |
| Community | Peers, families, mentors, and collaborators who strengthen the ecology. | Community |

## Voice

Write with warmth, precision, restraint, and respect for Sri Lankan realities. Explain practical value before philosophy. Do not romanticize hardship, teacher sacrifice, or rural life. Do not imply national-scale impact before evidence exists. Clearly distinguish research, interpretation, product vision, and early-stage observation.

Use “teaning” only after explaining it as the relational space where teaching and learning meet. Avoid making every user learn the term before understanding the product.

## Localization

Prepare English copy as translation-ready keys for Sinhala and Tamil. Avoid placing important meaning only inside images. Test mixed-script headings, line height, button widths, and long translated labels on low-width devices.

## Initial page responsibilities

- **Home:** say what Gurudevi is, who it supports, and where to begin.
- **About:** explain the ecology and the polycentric philosophy in accessible language.
- **Workspace:** explain Classroom, School, and Learning Studio without collapsing them into one role.
- **Library/Ideas:** connect product practice to EdLove research and philosophy.
- **FAQ:** answer practical questions about users, access, privacy, and stage of development.
- **Support:** collect help, feedback, bug reports, and feature requests.
- **Thuthimal:** explain gratitude and support without implying unverified impact.

## Content integrity

Do not publish fabricated numbers, testimonials, institutional logos, or claims of deployment. Use “Coming soon” or “Starting at zero” while public statistics are not verified. Philosophical and research work belongs canonically to EdLove; Gurudevi may summarize and link with attribution.
