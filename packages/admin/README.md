# @repo/admin — Institutional Stewardship

> **Compassionate school administration, institutional health, and transparent operational oversight.**

`@repo/admin` is the governance domain for school leaders, principals, and administrative stewards. It supports the complex organizational reality of running a school while maintaining transparency, equity, and human dignity.

---

## 🏛️ Real-World Stewardship Capabilities

* **School Structure & Sections**: Organizing school hierarchies, grade sections, classroom assignments, and academic terms in an intuitive, visual format.
* **Institutional Calendar**: Coordinating term dates, exams, community festivals, and school-wide events across all stakeholders.
* **Leave Approvals & Staff Care**: Respectful, streamlined workflows for managing educator and staff leave requests without bureaucratic friction.
* **Official Documentation & Letters**: Generating standard school letters, enrollment confirmations, and term summary reports easily and accurately.
* **Resource & Facility Visibility**: Tracking school laboratories, sports gear, libraries, and central educational supplies to ensure equitable distribution.

---

## 🧭 Philosophical & Architectural Invariants

* **Stewardship, Not Bureaucracy**: Software exists to lift drudgery from principals and clerks so they can focus on supporting teachers, students, and parents.
* **Non-Intrusion on Pedagogy**: Admin tools observe school health without micro-managing classroom teaching; pedagogical ownership remains with `@repo/classroom`.
* **Clear Human Placements**: Employee contracts, staff directory, and professional self-service live in `@repo/people`; physical infrastructure duties live in `@repo/workforce`.
* **Auditable Integrity**: Official actions produce verifiable receipts ensuring institutional accountability and community trust.
