# @repo/self — The Inner Sanctuary

> **A quiet, private haven for personal reflection, emotional balance, and inner renewal.**

`@repo/self` provides a dedicated space within the Gurudevi ecosystem for the inner life of both educators and students. Teaching and learning are deeply emotional human endeavors; sustainable education requires spaces for rest, reflection, and mindful awareness.

---

## 🌸 Key Capabilities for Wellbeing

* **Mindful Reflections & Journaling**: Private journal entries, daily gratitude notes, and guided thought records that help individuals process stress and celebrate moments of joy.
* **Energy & Rhythm Awareness**: Gentle self-check-ins allowing teachers and learners to notice their energy levels, emotional weather, and personal rhythms without judgment.
* **Role-Attuned Spaces**: Contextual reflection prompts tailored to the lived reality of teachers, students, and school leaders.
* **Complete Solitude & Privacy**: Reflections created here are sacredly private—they are never aggregated into administrative oversight scores, behavioral surveillance dashboards, or performance reviews.

---

## 🛡️ Trust & Psychological Safety Invariants

1. **Non-Extractive Design**: No streaks, guilt-inducing reminders, or gamified stress loops. You use the sanctuary when you need peace, and it welcomes you without judgment.
2. **Local Sovereignty**: Reflections remain on the user's personal device, protected by encryption and personal access boundaries.
3. **Restorative Pacing**: Visuals and interactions feature soft lighting, calming motion, and high legibility to reduce cognitive fatigue.

---

## 🤝 For Contributors

* Never propose features that broadcast personal emotional reflections to school management or external platforms.
* Visual styling in this package should emphasize warmth, spaciousness, and grounding.
