# @repo/classroom — The Learning Sanctuary

> **Where teacher presence, student growth, and classroom rhythm meet.**

`@repo/classroom` is the pedagogical heart of Gurudevi. It is purposefully engineered to support the living relationships inside a classroom—honoring the teacher as a mentor and the student as a curious, evolving learner.

---

## 🏫 Real-World Classroom Support

Rather than treating a classroom as a cold administrative database, this domain is shaped by the genuine day-to-day rhythm of school life:

* **Compassionate Attendance**: Fast, one-touch morning check-in that lets teachers greet every student by name rather than getting lost in paperwork.
* **Reflective Gradebook**: Assessment tools designed to illuminate student mastery and growth over time, avoiding punitive or high-stress scoring models.
* **Lesson Planning & Delivery**: Creative workspaces for teachers to organize lesson flows, learning materials, and practical activities.
* **Timetables & Daily Cadence**: Clear schedule overviews that keep classes oriented throughout the term.
* **Student Reflections & Portfolios**: Safe spaces where learners can express their reflections on lessons and share their work.

---

## 🌿 Living Architectural Invariants

* **Teacher-First Ergonomics**: Administrative steps are minimized so screen time never intrudes on human interaction.
* **Classroom Autonomy**: A classroom is an organic community with its own distinct culture, not just a row in an institutional spreadsheet.
* **Offline Ready**: Classroom records are fully accessible and editable even when the school's internet drops out.
* **Separation of Stewardship**: School-wide governance, official government correspondence, and principal operations belong in `@repo/admin`; `@repo/classroom` stays dedicated to teaching and learning.

---

## 🤝 For Contributors & Educators

When designing features for `@repo/classroom`:
* Ensure interactions are simple and high-contrast, functioning comfortably on teacher mobile phones as well as laptop browsers.
* Keep student feedback formative and encouraging.
* Respect that teachers know their students best—software should advise and assist, never dictate or displace human judgment.
