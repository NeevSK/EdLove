## Humane Vision & Purpose

AI in EdLove is strictly an instrument of teacher support and thoughtful self-reflection — never an autonomous evaluator or grading replacement. It assists with:
* Guided reflection prompts and mindfulness inquiries
* Lesson structuring suggestions for teachers
* Accessibility translation and contextual vocabulary assistance
