# @repo/workforce — Operational Staffing & School Services

> **Honoring and organizing the essential operational staff who make school life safe, functional, and welcoming.**

A school functions because of many dedicated hands beyond the classroom: laboratory assistants, office administrators, maintenance crews, library staff, security stewards, canteen workers, and transport teams. `@repo/workforce` provides clear organization for these indispensable operational services.

---

## 🛠️ Operational Domains

* **Operational Roles & Job Families**: Structured definition of service positions and duties across school facilities.
* **Service Duty Rosters**: Transparent duty scheduling for campus maintenance, lab preparation, and event logistics.
* **Campus Support Lines**: Clear reporting lines and service requests so teachers can request maintenance or lab support seamlessly.
* **Facility & Resource Alignment**: Connecting specific staff stewards with the facilities and tools they care for (science labs, IT rooms, sports fields).

---

## 🧭 Invariants & Governance

* **Parity of Esteem**: Non-academic staff are recognized as vital educational partners who co-create the school environment.
* **Built on Shared Identity**: Extends the human identity models in `@repo/people` without duplicating basic person records.
* **Clear Functional Scope**: This package models operational duties and facilities support; pedagogical teaching remains in `@repo/classroom` and institutional policy lives in `@repo/admin`.
