# @repo/payments — Transparent School Collections

> **Dignified school fee management, instant receipts, and transparent invoicing.**

`@repo/payments` provides clear, hassle-free tools for schools to manage student fees, lunch programs, excursion funds, and donations without administrative headaches or awkwardness for families.

---

## 💳 Core Capabilities

* **Clear Invoices & Statements**: Simple, easily understood fee breakdowns so parents always know exactly what they are contributing toward.
* **Instant Digital Receipts**: Immediate verifiable receipts issued via SMS, WhatsApp, or printable cards upon fee payment.
* **Flexible Payment Workflows**: Accommodates cash, local bank transfers, and digital payment methods with equal ease.
* **Fee Assistance & Waivers**: Dignified workflows for recording community scholarships, hardship waivers, and subsidies without singling out students.

---

## 🛡️ Invariants

* **Compassionate Collection**: Never locks students out of learning materials or educational access due to pending family payments.
* **Offline Recording**: School bursars can record cash payments and issue paper-matched numbers even when offline; records synchronize once the internet is restored.
* **Downstream Integration**: Reconciles cleanly with the general ledger in `@repo/accounting`.
