# @repo/form-builder — Community Sensing & Forms

> **Intuitive form design and community feedback collection for schools and researchers.**

`@repo/form-builder` provides simple, flexible tools for educators, researchers, and administrators to gather feedback, conduct field surveys, and capture classroom observations without writing code.

---

## 📝 Core Capabilities

* **Classroom & School Surveys**: Collecting student sentiment, learning climate observations, and parent feedback effortlessly.
* **Offline Field Data Collection**: Field workers and educators can fill out multi-step forms in remote areas with zero network connectivity; entries are safely saved locally.
* **Accessible Inputs**: Form controls support large touch targets, clear screen-reader labels, and multi-language input prompts (Sinhala, Tamil, English).
* **Flexible Field Types**: Rating scales, multi-choice selectors, rich text, signatures, and photographic attachments.

---

## 🧭 Design Invariants

* **Clarity Over Complexity**: Questions and answer options remain clean and easy to comprehend, reducing cognitive load on students and respondents.
* **Privacy & Consent**: Form responses respect explicit respondent consent; sensitive data is safeguarded according to strict ethical guidelines.
