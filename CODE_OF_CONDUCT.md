# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- **Be kind, precise, and evidence-backed.** Review with file:line references,
  not vibes (`AGENTS.md` — Verification discipline).
- **No surveillance.** Student PII is never used for ranking or tracking outside
  explicit, consented flows.
- **Scope matters.** A 2-file task should not touch 9 files (`AGENTS.md` — No scope creep).

Enforcement: `conduct@edlove.org`. Reports are confidential; retaliation is not tolerated.

For escalation, contact the maintainer listed in `LICENSE` (NeevSK).
