## Why This Exists

**Gurudevi** is an offline-first education platform deployed in Sri Lankan schools:

- **Teachers** use it for attendance, assignments, grades, guardian communication
- **Students** use it for curriculum library (PDF/video/ebook reader), wellbeing journaling (mood lotus, fire journal, voice journal, sleep tracker, gratitude, goals)
- **Principals** use it for governance history, resonance patterns, monthly reflection
- **Guardians** use it for student progress, classroom visibility

**AI is our largest cost** — voice journal transcription (Whisper), guided reflection (GPT-4o/Claude), OCR/document tools, OpenRouter fallback.

$500/mo covers the AI bill for a pilot school. $2,000/mo covers a district.
